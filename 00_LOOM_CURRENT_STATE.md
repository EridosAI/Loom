## Accepted functional branch through Fork 28

**[JASON-ACCEPTED WORKING]** The functional design is accepted through Fork 28 at the
following high level:

- a continuous generative causal simulation, with physical reality retained as an escalation;
- a bounded, continuous, non-wrapping 2D world;
- a persistent oriented compact organism with paired low-level actuation;
- directional two-band light and two-component diffusive chemical sensing;
- contact, proprioception, and interoception;
- structured spontaneous activity;
- energy and integrity as separate viability dimensions;
- a finite renewable energy-source ecology and distinct world-mediated integrity restoration;
- one periodic, non-agentive moving block;
- reusable materials and materially homogeneous simple rigid convex bodies;
- no general pushable furniture in the baseline;
- anchored extended restorative surfaces; and
- multiple resource and restorative opportunities considered relative to world area.

Exact numerical configuration remains open.
