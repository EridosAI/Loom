# Mapping Existing Mechanisms Against a Twelve-Characteristic Functional Specification for a Central "Association Cortex" Operation

## TL;DR
- **No single existing architecture satisfies the full specification, and the most valuable finding is a confirmed three-way gap: no published mechanism simultaneously delivers (6) controllable decay-as-dynamics, (8) tolerance of a drifting input space favouring recent material, and (10) modality-agnostic perception/action-blind evocation.** The field splits into three corners — drift/decay models (6+8), perception–action common-coding models (10), and associative-memory models with forgetting (6 + partial 10) — and no work bridges all three.
- **Strong synergies exist around a small number of mechanism-families:** attractor/associative dynamics (covers 2, 3, 5, and the persistence question), predictive-coding/active-inference (covers 3, 7, 10, 11, 12), and Hebbian-plus-decay plasticity (covers 5, 6, 7). The biggest tensions are between sequence-storing asymmetric attractors (needed for 3/4) and graceful drift-tolerance (8), and between active inference's stable generative model (10) and first-class representational drift (6/8).
- **Two of the open questions resolve toward the spec's working assumptions; two stay genuinely open.** Within-wave (2) and across-wave (3) calling-forth are naturally one mechanism in attractor and predictive-coding accounts (symmetric vs. asymmetric weights in the *same* network), and multi-step look-ahead is most parsimoniously the single-step operation iterated. But where persistence lives, and whether the called-forth unit is a bundle or finer, remain unforced by the evidence.

## Key Findings

**1. Most individual characteristics are well-served by mature literatures.** Eleven of the twelve have at least one strong mechanism-level candidate with primary-source backing. Wave-bundling maps onto theta–gamma phase–amplitude coupling and discrete perceptual frames; cued calling-forth maps onto modern Hopfield/dense associative memory and predictive-coding associative memory; strengthening/weakening map onto attractor-basin deepening and synaptic decay; no-phase-separation maps onto online/local-plasticity learning; modality-agnostic evocation maps onto common-coding/active inference; and the developmental characteristics (9, 11, 12) map onto progressive differentiation, predictive-processing development, and learned multisensory integration.

**2. The single hardest characteristic in isolation is (6)'s requirement that drift/decay be a first-class, cortex-independent control parameter.** Most decay mechanisms (weight decay, homeostatic plasticity, synaptic depression) are *coupled* to the learning rule and the representational substrate. The spec demands decay be a knob that is independent of any cortex's update rule — the closest realisations are Ornstein–Uhlenbeck drift models and the active-inference Dirichlet "forgetting rate" ω, but both attach decay to the substrate they operate on.

**3. The 6+8+10 triad is the genuine architectural gap (highest-priority finding).** Drift/decay-tolerant associative dynamics (6+8) are almost always formulated in a *single shared* representation space, which conflicts with (10)'s requirement that the central unit be blind to modality and never project into a common space. Conversely, the cleanest perception/action-blind mechanisms (active inference, common coding) assume a *stable generative model* whose structure does not itself drift. No published mechanism unifies all three.

## Details

### Per-Characteristic Map

**(1) Wave-bundling — co-arrival = co-occurrence, no finer timing.**
- *Theta–gamma phase–amplitude coupling* is the strongest neural analogue. The canonical formulation is Lisman & Jensen, "The Theta-Gamma Neural Code" (*Neuron* 77(6):1002–1016, 2013): a slow theta cycle holds roughly seven nested gamma subcycles (4–8 items, tied to Miller's 7±2), each gamma slot packaging one co-occurring item. Critically, Jensen & Lisman (2000) showed that **phase distinctions finer than a gamma cycle do not improve reconstruction accuracy** — i.e., there is no useful timestamp finer than the slot, directly matching "the wave is the unit of now." Auditory evidence for theta-framing/gamma-packaging appears in the Giraud–Poeppel speech-sampling line (*Human Brain Mapping*, 2023).
- *Discrete perceptual frames / rhythmic sampling* (VanRullen's discrete perception; rhythmic attention) supports the claim that perception is sampled in cycles rather than continuously.
- *Event segmentation theory* (Zacks, Reynolds, Baldwin; computational model by Reynolds et al. 2007; Bayesian-surprise model, Kumar et al. *Cognitive Science* 2023) supplies the complementary idea that boundaries between "nows" arise from transient prediction-error increases — relevant because the wave-to-wave transition is where surprise is computed.
- *Stance:* binding-by-co-arrival is implementation-light; it requires only a sampling clock, which the "wave" supplies by fiat. No persistence stance implied.

**(2) Cued calling-forth, within-wave (pattern completion / hetero-association).**
- *Modern Hopfield networks / dense associative memory* (Krotov & Hopfield 2016, NeurIPS; Ramsauer et al. 2020, "Hopfield Networks is All You Need," arXiv:2008.02217) perform content-addressable completion: given part of a pattern, settle to the whole. Hetero-associative variants associate distinct patterns (cross-modal: sight-of-bell → sound).
- *Universal Hopfield Networks* (Millidge et al. 2022) unify these as similarity→separation→projection, showing auto- and hetero-association are one family.
- *Associative memories via predictive coding* (Salvatori, Song, Bogacz et al. 2021, NeurIPS) does multimodal hetero-association (retrieve image from description and vice versa) within a hierarchical generative network.
- *Stance:* implies persistence is *folded into* the operation — the associations ARE the weights/energy landscape; no separate store needed. Assumes a shared vector space (tension with 10, below).

**(3) Cued calling-forth, across-wave (successor / sequence prediction).**
- *Successor representation* (Stachenfeld, Botvinick & Gershman 2017; Dayan 1993) predicts a discounted future-state occupancy; empirically supported as skewing representations toward successors (Ekman et al.; "Successor-like representation guides prediction," *eLife* 2023, PMC9894584).
- *Asymmetric Hopfield / sequence attractors* (Sompolinsky & Kanter 1986; "Long Sequence Hopfield Memory," Chaudhry et al. 2023, arXiv:2306.04532; "Learning Sequence Attractors in Recurrent Networks with Hidden Neurons," arXiv:2404.02729) store cup→kettle→pour transitions via temporally asymmetric weights in the *same* network that does (2)'s symmetric completion.
- *Predictive coding / forward models* and *hippocampal replay/preplay* (sequence reinstatement) also fit.
- **Open question (2 vs 3):** The attractor literature shows these can be *one* mechanism — symmetric weight terms produce within-wave completion, asymmetric terms produce across-wave succession, in a single network ("MixedNet," Chaudhry et al.). This LEANS toward "same mechanism, two weight regimes," but does not force it.

**(4) Tracing — called-forth bundle becomes next cue (multi-step chains).**
- *Autoregressive recurrent rollout* in sequence attractors and world models naturally iterates (3) one step at a time.
- *Hippocampal replay/preplay* generates internally-driven multi-step trajectories without external prompting (Foster & Wilson 2006; predictive replay literature).
- **Open question (single-step iterated vs one-shot trajectory):** World-model evidence strongly favours iteration but warns of *compounding error*. This is formalized in the compounding-error/exposure-bias literature: per Yann LeCun's 2023 argument (discussed in arXiv:2505.24187), autoregressive models "drift out of distribution" with each step, with the probability of a fully correct sequence falling as (1−e)^n in sequence length n — approaching zero for sufficiently large n — a behavior traced to Bengio et al. 2015's "exposure bias." This is exactly the spec's note that reach is limited by error accumulation, and supports the "one operation iterated" regime. Candidates that emit a whole ordered trajectory in one act (e.g., sequence-to-sequence emission) assume the *distinct-operation* regime and should be flagged as such.

**(5) Strengthening by recurrence — confidence as graded property of evocation.**
- *Attractor-basin deepening:* repeated patterns carve deeper, steeper basins; confidence is read out as basin steepness/settling speed, NOT a stored score. Direct neural evidence: Wang, Falcone, Richmond & Averbeck, "Attractor dynamics reflect decision confidence in macaque prefrontal cortex" (*Nature Neuroscience* 26:1970–1980, 2023, DOI 10.1038/s41593-023-01445-x). In two rhesus monkeys making accept/reject decisions, "the attractor basins around patterns of activity reflecting decisions had steeper landscapes for offers that led to consistent decisions" — choices were consistent for very good/very bad offers and less consistent for intermediate offers — operationalising confidence as an emergent property of the landscape rather than a stored value.
- *Hebbian plasticity / repetition effects* and *synaptic consolidation* provide the strengthening substrate.
- *Stance:* persistence folded into dynamics; confidence is emergent, satisfying the spec's "graded property of the evocation itself."

**(6) Weakening by disuse — decay as first-class, cortex-independent parameter.**
- *Synaptic weight decay / passive forgetting* and *homeostatic plasticity* (synaptic homeostasis hypothesis, Tononi & Cirelli; "Decay happens," Hardt, Nader & Nadel, *Trends in Cognitive Sciences* 2013) supply the fading-by-disuse dynamic.
- *Active/adaptive forgetting* ("Adaptive Synaptic Plasticity," arXiv:1703.07655 — explicit exponential decay + recovery; retrieval-induced forgetting, PMC6220340) frames forgetting as a controllable process, not deletion.
- *Representational-drift-as-storage* models cast decay as an Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process with an explicit time-constant ("Representational drift as the consequence of ongoing memory storage," *Scientific Reports* 2025).
- *Active inference* offers a tunable **forgetting rate ω** on Dirichlet concentration parameters (Smith, Friston & Whyte 2022 tutorial) and structural pruning via Bayesian model reduction (Smith et al. 2020, PMC7250191) — the closest realisation of decay as an explicit knob.
- **No good fit flag:** the spec wants decay *independent of any cortex's update rule*. Every candidate above attaches decay to the substrate it lives on. The active-inference ω is the nearest, but it decays *parameters/counts*, not a free-standing representation space.

**(7) No phase separation — every wave uses and adjusts structure.**
- *Online/continual learning without task boundaries* ("New Insights for the Stability-Plasticity Dilemma," arXiv:2302.08741; streaming learning, UPGD arXiv:2404.00781).
- *Local plasticity rules operating during inference* (STDP, predictive-coding local updates; "Continual Learning with Hebbian Plasticity in Sparse and Predictive Coding Networks," arXiv:2407.17305; neuromodulated local error signals, arXiv:2007.08159).
- *Predictive coding* inherently learns-while-inferring: the same prediction-error signal drives both perception and weight update.
- *Synergy:* this is essentially free in attractor and predictive-coding substrates, which update continuously. Conflicts with anything requiring a replay/consolidation phase (CLS, below) unless replay is interleaved online.

**(8) Tolerance of a moving space — usable associations as inputs drift, favouring recent material.**
- *Drift-robust readout* literature: "Stable task information from an unstable neural population" (*eLife* 2019, PMC51121); "Self-healing codes" (Rule & O'Leary, PNAS 2022, PMC8851551) — internally-generated signals track drift via homeostatic plasticity; "Coordinated representational drift supports stable place coding" (bioRxiv 2025) — drift is a structured translation/rotation preserving population geometry, enabling stable downstream readout; "Statistics of cortical representational drift can enable robust readout" (bioRxiv 2024).
- *Stance:* These show downstream readouts CAN stay stable under drift, but almost all assume the *readout and the drifting code share a space* or that the readout continually recalibrates with plasticity. Favouring recent material is automatic if decay (6) runs concurrently — recent associations sit where the space currently is.
- **Synergy with (6):** decay + drift together naturally implement recency-favouring. **Tension with (10):** see below.

**(9) Refinement by splitting — coarse association divides into finer ones without global rebuild.**
- *Progressive differentiation* (Rogers & McClelland semantic cognition; Saxe, McClelland & Ganguli 2013/2019 — deep linear nets provably differentiate coarse→fine from hierarchical statistics).
- *Coarse-to-fine representation learning* (developmental and CNN spatial-frequency work, PMC8458991).
- *Dynamic capacity expansion / neuron splitting:* Dynamically Expandable Networks (Yoon et al. 2018, arXiv:1708.01547) literally *split/duplicate* a neuron when its role drifts past threshold — a direct mechanistic analogue of "one ball-association becoming red-ball and green-ball." Neurogenesis-style methods (Neurogenesis Deep Learning; "Self-Motivated Growing Neural Network," arXiv:2512.12713) add capacity without task boundaries.
- *Stance:* DEN's split requires a (local, not global) retraining step; whether this counts as "without a global rebuild" depends on locality. SOM-style splitting and progressive differentiation are more naturally incremental.

**(10) Native-slice consumption — modality-agnostic, perception/action-blind evocation.**
- *Common coding theory* (Prinz; Hommel et al. "Theory of Event Coding") and *ideomotor theory* (James): actions are coded by their perceptual effects; the same code serves perceiving and enacting.
- *Active inference* (Adams, Shipp & Friston 2013, "Predictions not commands," *Brain Structure and Function* 218(3):611–643): motor commands ARE proprioceptive predictions; action and perception are the same inference, with the command/prediction distinction living at the periphery (reflex arcs), exactly matching "the distinction lives in the cortex, not the association cortex."
- *Active Predictive Coding* (Rao 2024, "A sensory–motor theory of the neocortex," *Nature Neuroscience* 27(7):1221–1235): each cortical area estimates both sensory states and actions in one scheme; coupled to a hippocampus-like associative memory it binds multimodal activations.
- *Stance:* These are the best fit for (10), but they assume a *generative model* and, in cross-modal forms, a common latent space. This is the crux of the central tension.

**(11) Growing reach — from corrective-only to priming-before-arrival.**
- *Developmental predictive processing:* infants show prediction/priming that matures with experience and motor development (Köster et al. 2020, *Perspectives on Psychological Science*; Schwarzer 2024, *Child Development Perspectives*; neonatal top-down prediction, PMC12750968; "Young Infants Process Prediction Errors at the Theta Rhythm").
- *Anticipatory vs corrective prediction:* expectation biases perception before arrival (de Lange, Kok line; successor-skewing in V1, *eLife* 2023).
- *Error accumulation in rollouts* (world-model literature, above) explains why reach is bounded and grows only as the cortices/tracing become more accurate — matching the spec's note that reach growth is NOT longer emitted sequences but lower per-step error.
- *Extensibility flag:* predictive-coding/active-inference candidates extend naturally to priming (the prediction just gains precision/weight) and to surfacing-for-arbitration (predicted states can feed a downstream selector). A pure pattern-completer with no temporal forward model is a strong early predictor that CANNOT be extended toward priming — flag as the "wrong choice even if it wins early."

**(12) Coherence as learned, not imposed.**
- *Multisensory integration as learned:* self-supervised audio-visual alignment (Owens & Efros 2018, "Audio-Visual Scene Analysis with Self-Supervised Multisensory Features," arXiv:1804.03641 — learns from detecting misalignment); developmental multisensory recalibration (temporal synchrony as the early "glue"; *Cognition* and *Scientific Reports* work).
- *Binding through prediction-error minimisation:* cross-modal incoherence is surprising and thus corrected; integration emerges because mismatch carries error (active-inference multisensory accounts).
- *Stance:* directly supports "incoherence is surprising, integration is learned." Synergises with (7) and (10).

### Synergy Map (one mechanism, several characteristics)
- **Attractor / associative dynamics (modern Hopfield + asymmetric sequence terms):** covers (2) completion, (3) succession, (4) iterated tracing, (5) basin-deepening confidence, and folds persistence into its own weights (relevant to the persistence open question). One substrate, five characteristics.
- **Predictive coding / active inference:** covers (3) prediction, (7) learn-while-infer, (10) perception/action common code, (11) priming, (12) learned coherence. The most synergistic single family for the *developmental and action* half of the spec.
- **Hebbian-plus-decay plasticity:** covers (5) strengthening, (6) weakening, and (7) always-on adjustment together.
- **Theta–gamma / event-segmentation:** covers (1) bundling and supplies the surprise signal used in (11)/(12).

### Tension Map (MOST IMPORTANT — where satisfying one breaks another)

- **6+8+10 (the central gap, confirmed).** Mechanisms that handle decay+drift (6+8) — modern Hopfield with weight decay, OU-drift readout models — are formulated in a *single shared* d-dimensional space with a common similarity metric. Hetero-associative completion typically reduces to auto-association on a *concatenated joint vector* (BayesPCN, Yoo & Wood 2022, arXiv:2205.09930: hetero-association is implemented by "forming a 'joint' object … combining the key and value into one object"). That shared/joint-space assumption directly conflicts with (10)'s demand that the central unit be blind to modality and never project cortices into a common space. Conversely, the cleanest (10) mechanisms — active inference, common coding, Active Predictive Coding — assume a *stable generative model* whose structure does not itself drift; their decay (active-inference ω, Bayesian model reduction) acts on slow parameters, not on a continuously drifting embedding. **No single published model unites OU-style recency-favouring drift, a controllable decay knob, AND a modality-agnostic perception/action code.** The closest partial bridges: BayesPCN (6 + partial 10, no action side), Rao's Active Predictive Coding (10 + partial cross-modal binding, no decay/drift knob), OU-drift storage models (6+8, no action), and active-inference-with-ω (6 + 10, but parameter-decay not embedding-drift). This is the deliverable's headline: the triad is an open architectural problem.

- **3/4 (sequence attractors) vs 8 (graceful drift).** Asymmetric sequence attractors store transitions in precise weight structure tuned to *fixed* pattern vectors; when the input space drifts (8), stored transitions point to stale locations. Sequence-capacity scaling laws (Chaudhry et al. 2023) assume stationary patterns. Drift-tolerance and high-fidelity sequence storage pull in opposite directions.

- **5 (deepen attractors) vs 6 (let them fade) vs 9 (split them).** Deepening a basin (5) entrenches it against the decay (6) and against splitting (9) — a deep basin resists differentiation into red-ball/green-ball. This is a three-way stability–plasticity tension; the active-inference observation that Dirichlet priors "become increasingly entrenched with experience … reducing flexibility unless … parameter decay is introduced" (arXiv:2505.03308) captures it precisely.

- **7 (no phase separation) vs CLS-style consolidation (persistence).** Complementary Learning Systems (McClelland, McNaughton & O'Reilly 1995; Kumaran, Hassabis & McClelland 2016) solves persistence and graceful integration via a fast hippocampal store replayed offline into slow cortex — but replay is classically an *offline phase*, conflicting with (7). Only online-interleaved-replay variants escape this.

- **2 (shared-bundle completion) vs 10 (separate cortex spaces).** Within-wave completion across modalities (sight-of-bell → sound) is easiest if all summaries share a space; (10) forbids that. HEN (Kashyap et al. 2024, arXiv:2409.16408) is the closest published case of binding vectors from *separate* encoder spaces (CLIP-text ↔ D-VAE-image) without first projecting to a common embedding — but it still requires each item to live in *some* encoded latent space and relies on associations being unique. Fully metric-free, modality-agnostic binding does not appear to exist.

### Explicit "No Good Fit Found" Flags
1. **(6) decay as a substrate-independent first-class parameter:** no mechanism cleanly separates the decay knob from the representation it acts on. Active-inference ω is nearest but parameter-bound.
2. **6+8+10 jointly:** no single or tightly-coupled mechanism-family. Genuine gap.
3. **Modality-agnostic associative binding with no common space:** HEN relaxes but does not eliminate the encoded-space requirement; a truly metric-free version is unattested.
4. **Decay-tolerant high-capacity sequence storage:** sequence attractors assume stationary patterns; no candidate combines long-sequence capacity with graceful drift.

### Open Questions (kept open, candidates tagged by stance)
- **Are (2) and (3) one mechanism or two?** OPEN. Attractor accounts (symmetric vs asymmetric terms in one network) and predictive coding (same generative model, spatial vs temporal prediction) both permit *one* mechanism — these candidates assume the *unified* stance. Separate hetero-association vs a distinct sequence module would assume *two*. Evidence leans unified but does not force it.
- **Single-step iterated vs one-shot trajectory (multi-step look-ahead).** OPEN but leaning iterated. Autoregressive attractor rollout and replay assume *iterated single-step* (and inherit compounding error, characteristic 11's limiter, formalized by the exposure-bias literature). Sequence-to-sequence emission assumes *distinct one-shot trajectory*. Tag candidates accordingly; the spec's paradigm and the error-accumulation evidence both favour iterated.
- **What is the unit of the called-forth thing?** OPEN. Bundle-of-summaries is the working assumption; nothing in the evidence fixes granularity. Hierarchical associative memory (Krotov 2021) and hierarchical predictive coding permit finer or coarser units.
- **Where does persistence live?** OPEN. Attractor/Hopfield and predictive-coding-associative candidates fold persistence *into the operation's own structure* (the associations ARE the weights) — agnostic-friendly. CLS and hippocampal-index theory (Teyler & DiScenna) imply a *separate store*. Both are viable; the spec can stay agnostic by choosing a substrate where persistence is the weight matrix itself.
- **Downstream arbitration / extensibility.** Predictive-coding/active-inference candidates extend toward priming (11) and toward surfacing-for-arbitration (predicted states feed a selector). Pure single-shot pattern-completers do not extend to priming and should be rejected for the mature system even if they win the early corrective task.

## Recommendations
1. **Treat the 6+8+10 triad as the design's central research risk, not a solved component.** Stage the build: first implement (10) modality-agnostic evocation on a *quasi-stationary* space (Active Predictive Coding or active-inference common coding), then introduce controllable decay (active-inference ω or explicit weight decay), then stress-test under induced drift. The benchmark that would change the approach: if recency-favouring recall degrades sequence fidelity below task threshold under drift, the shared-space assumption must be abandoned for a per-cortex-space binding scheme (HEN-style) despite its uniqueness limitation.
2. **Adopt an attractor-with-asymmetric-terms substrate for (2)–(5) as the leading hypothesis,** because it unifies completion, succession, tracing, and graded confidence in one network and folds persistence into weights — directly addressing the persistence open question without committing to a separate store. Threshold to revisit: if sequence capacity under drift collapses (the 3/4-vs-8 tension), add hidden-neuron sequence attractors or a CLS-style fast store with *online interleaved* replay (never an offline phase, to preserve characteristic 7).
3. **Use prediction-error magnitude as the universal currency** tying (1) bundling/segmentation, (11) priming-vs-correcting, and (12) learned coherence together. This is the most synergistic single design decision and is well-supported across event-segmentation and active-inference literatures.
4. **For (9) refinement, prototype neuron-splitting (DEN-style) gated by local drift thresholds rather than global retraining,** and verify locality empirically; fall back to progressive-differentiation dynamics if splitting forces global rebuilds.
5. **Reject any candidate for the core operation that cannot extend to priming and arbitration,** even strong early predictors — per the extensibility flag.

## Caveats
- Several key sources are 2024–2026 preprints (bioRxiv/arXiv) not yet peer-reviewed, including the OU-drift-as-storage and "predictive forgetting" papers and Active Predictive Coding's preprint version; treat their specific quantitative claims as provisional.
- The spec's deliberately neutral vocabulary means some mappings are interpretive: e.g., whether DEN's local retraining counts as "without a global rebuild," or whether active-inference parameter decay counts as "drift on the representation space," are judgment calls flagged in-text.
- "Binding-by-synchrony" as a literal neural solution to characteristic (1) remains theoretically contested. Even within the theta–gamma framework, Lisman noted that "the temporal synchrony postulated to encode an item is not precise," and that gamma slots may drift rather than act as a strict clock (Burns et al. 2011; related *eLife* work, 2015). The spec sidesteps this debate by defining the wave as an *imposed* sampling clock rather than an emergent synchrony — the more defensible engineering choice.
- This map prioritises mechanism-level and review sources over popular summaries, but coverage is necessarily uneven across twelve large literatures; absence of a cited candidate for a sub-claim reflects search scope, not proof of non-existence.
