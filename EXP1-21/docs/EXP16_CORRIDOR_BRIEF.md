## §6 CONDUCT IN THE CORRIDOR

Report-don't-patch stands — a surprising-but-in-envelope value is recorded and the corridor continues; an out-of-envelope value halts. No constant, definition, or route may be modified in-corridor for any reason. Mechanical folds (typo-class, cited) are permitted with record; anything touching a number, a route, or a definition is judgment-class. The wrong-reason taxonomy applies to the corridor itself: a gate passed for an unverifiable reason is a halt, not a pass.
