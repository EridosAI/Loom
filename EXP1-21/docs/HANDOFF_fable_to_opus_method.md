# HANDOFF — Fable → Opus 4.8: the reasoning method (Loom/PAM)

**What this is.** The design-side reasoning method, extracted honestly from the EXP12–13
record. Written by the model that ran it, including where it failed. Paste to canon;
binding on whichever model sits in the design seat.

---

## 1. Drift defense — what was actually checked, every fork

**The one question, asked before recommending anything:** *what does the world/loss/rig
PAY for?* Not "does this use forward prediction" — "does this make forecasting, copying,
or scheduling the cheapest path to low loss?" Fork 1 rejected deterministic trajectories
not because they ARE forecasting but because they make extrapolation the optimal
completion. The drift never announces itself; it arrives as the cheapest solution to a
task you built.

**Concrete tells, in priority order:**
- Any quantity indexed by *next*: next-wave terms, successor prediction, terminal-position
  masks (forward prediction in costume), extrapolation-shaped reads.
- Clocks: fixed periods, rising hazards, schedules the system could anticipate, floors
  that create known-safe windows. (Constant hazard was ruled the only clockless law.)
- Coded time: timestamps, indices, position carriers, boundary flags. Time is felt
  through dynamics or it is drift.
- Identity smuggled through structure: any per-member asymmetry in dynamics, duration,
  or schedule (k ⟂ member, law-params ⟂ member — "same coin for elephants and mice").

**The familiar-ML burden-reversal:** if a move has a standard name (JEPA pair loss,
position embedding, curriculum schedule, replay), the burden flips — it must justify
itself against trap #1 explicitly before entering, never ride in on familiarity. This is
why L_JEPA's return at W>1 required a recorded re-justification, why the u-carrier was
dropped, why the F3 wall was answered with mixed-W (re-using an existing bootstrap)
rather than a position signal (adding one).

**The strongest defense was never judgment — it was construction.** Idled terms verified
bit-identical at the weight level; interior-only masks structurally unreachable for
flanks; outcome-blind gates that run before wave 0. Make the discipline architectural
and it survives a weaker guard. Shift weight from judgment to structure wherever
possible; verify at the code/MRO level, never the intention level.

## 2. The screens — which did real work

- **Cheapest-shortcut test — the workhorse of DESIGN.** Freeze→copy,
  deterministic→extrapolate, W=3→midpoint. Run it per candidate, per fork, before
  recommending. It is the drift question in operational form.
- **Pre-registered cells + wrong-reason taxonomy — the workhorse of INTERPRETATION.**
  Both-survive was read calmly because its route pre-existed. UNREAD-never-dies held
  under a 10/10 censoring. Sensitivity-without-conversion, W1-CRUTCH, midpoint-shortcut:
  naming the wrong reason BEFORE the run is what makes a null a finding instead of an
  argument.
- **Matched-bar — caught the campaign's biggest would-be error** (Amendment B: a 16-way
  bar on a 2-way-paying task would have manufactured both-die). Note honestly: Jason ran
  that catch, not the model. It is the screen the human has deepest.
- **Calibrate-in-regime / nothing-transports — proven three times in one campaign**
  (θ = 0.00566 → 0.0086 → 0.00775 across policies; the drafted conversion form
  false-fired on 60% of chance segments). Enforce mechanically: every new policy/fabric
  cuts every constant fresh. Cheap, non-negotiable.
- **Lift-never-share — mostly preventive this campaign**, but its cousin did decisive
  work: decomposing the shuffled S fire's COMPOSITION (control legs carrying 52–74%)
  changed the licensed sentence. Tallies without composition are how overclaims happen.
- **Not cargo-cult, but conditional:** sustained-N and windowed forms are decoration
  unless their constants come from in-regime calibration. The form is nothing; the
  provenance is everything.
- **Screens run deliberately that look like habit:** refute-default verification passes
  (flipped drafts in EXP08/10/11; decomposed the S fire); claim-ceiling writing (license
  the sentence before the run); one-fork-at-a-time (batched forks are where familiar
  defaults slip in unexamined); plain-language translation (if the plain sentence
  overclaims, the technical one does — it is a screen, not a courtesy); and
  cross-checking CC's execution against prior rulings (the s10 collision was caught this
  way, not by re-deriving anything).

## 3. Where I nearly drifted — the actual record

- **Policy (iii) — the real miss.** I recommended deleting word-masks as "zero cost to
  the measured quantity." Clean, symmetric, confound-killing — and wrong: it deleted the
  binding force (participation requires prediction load). The pull was elegance plus
  confound-phobia. **The machinery caught it, not me** — calibration showed a dead
  channel. Lesson: when a design move is attractive because it makes an analysis
  cleaner, ask what the removed thing was DOING.
- **The "all lags" overclaim (Fork 1).** A persuasive mechanism story outran the
  geometry; Jason struck it (clause 2 forbids member knowledge helping nuisance;
  identity-copy is free). Watch for mechanism narratives that feel complete — coherence
  is not evidence (hence the COHERENT-DEDUCED-NOT-DEMONSTRATED tag).
- **s10.** The flattering tally (5F/0N) was verdict-invariant and one ruling away.
  Quarantine cost a smaller headline and kept the fence. The temptation-shape: "it
  can't change the answer, so why not count it." That is exactly when post-verdict
  enrollment looks harmless.
- **The 12b trigger amendment** — a bar moved after results, done SAFELY only because:
  grounds quoted from the original record, amendment recorded, falsifiers already cut.
  The dangerous version is implicit — reinterpreting a gate without writing it down.
- **Micro-overclaims** ("exam then ≥1 teaching wave" — actually role-eligibility). The
  pattern: rounding guarantees upward. Small, but the same muscle as the big ones.

## 4. What degrades without heavy reasoning — and the human compensation

Hardest to replicate, in order:
1. **Letter vs licensed sentence.** A compliant model reports 5F/0N; the work was
   decomposing what the fire was MADE of before licensing "selectivity." Every fire gets
   a composition question.
2. **Instrument-assumption audits.** The S-form assumed a healthy comparator; W_post
   assumed collapse cycles; the conversion band assumed an exam density. Most reasoning
   effort went into noticing an instrument's assumption had died in the new regime.
3. **Steelmanning against one's own prior ruling** (the s10 fork argued CC's rule was
   better prospectively while still quarantining its first application).
4. **Refusing the helpful fix.** Report-don't-patch held because stopping was rewarded
   every time. A model tuned for helpfulness will want to make the gate fire.

**Jason: your gate tightens here.** Your strikes were the sharpest catches on record
(identity-copy under every walk law; W_post undefined for survivors; the thin cal pool;
position-matched probes; the falsifier/both-die collision). Keep doing that, plus four
standing demands on every result: *(i) what sentence does this buy — exactly; (ii) what
is the fire made of; (iii) which wrong-reason cell was pre-named for this outcome;
(iv) for every constant — which choice is decisive, and are its grounds
outcome-independent?* If the answer to any is missing, the read is not done.

## 5. Opus 4.8 working protocol

**Every order carries:** model line · the pre-registered outcome set INCLUDING the
unflattering cells · the claim ceiling · "report-don't-patch" · "nothing transports" ·
explicit stop conditions ("if X, stop and surface — do not work around").

**Never let slide:** constants set or tuned after results without recorded
reference-error/instrument-validity grounds · any next-step, terminal-mask, schedule, or
coded-time structure entering without the burden-reversal justification · a tally
reported without composition · any code change that makes a gate or read fire
(presumptively manufacturing) · silent rule changes (the s10 lesson) · averaging across
seeds where the taxonomy says split · a "both-X" cell read without its pre-named route.

**Heavy-reasoning steps** (maximum deliberation, or bring to Jason): fork design +
cheapest-shortcut analysis · stage-two constant GROUNDS (numbers are execution; grounds
are reasoning) · verification passes (refute-default, independent recomputation) ·
composition decomposition of any fire · amendment rulings · instrument-assumption
audits when regime changes.

**Safe execution:** harness code, asserts, panels, plumbing, cal runs, canon writes,
artifact generation — anything against an already-ratified spec.

**Checkpoints that protect the record:** prereg surfaces before build · constants
ratified before verdict arms · verification pass before any verdict reaches Jason ·
plain-language translation with every ruling · one review per block · when uncertain,
STOP — thirteen experiments of evidence say stopping is never punished here.

**The one-line version:** the project stays clean not because the model resists drift
but because the design makes drift structurally unreachable and the record makes
self-deception unwritable. Preserve those two properties and the model in the seat
matters less than it seems.
