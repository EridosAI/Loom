## §1 What the solved loop actually is

A cortex that converts is not a benchmark pass — it is a **module that binds cross-modal identity under coherent experience**, shipped with its operating manual: the experience regime it needs (the reality ladder's minimal recipe), the shortcuts it must be denied (the rung taxonomy), and possibly the law that protects it (surprise-gated plasticity, §4). Everything below treats that module as the primitive.

## §2 Multi-cortex: cascading stability (Jason's frame, sharpened)

**The two-cortex case has been running all along.** The word channel *is* a perfectly rigid pre-trained cortex — clean labels, zero drift — and the entire campaign has been "plastic cortex against rigid teacher." Multi-cortex is the *controlled relaxation* of that rigidity, and the known failure mode is mutual chase: two equally plastic cortices teaching each other collapse or oscillate (the reason the JEPA lineage needs EMA teachers — an architectural stability asymmetry).

**Three implementations of the cascade, one rule:** scheduled (freeze order — brittle, curriculum-fragile) · architectural (EMA-style asymmetry — proven elsewhere, imports a design commitment) · **emergent via the gating law** — confidence-ordered plasticity makes mature cortices de facto teachers automatically; this is CWP's claim 1 wearing multi-cortex clothes, and it is the version that scales past two. The rule under all three: **no two equally-plastic cortices may be each other's primary teacher.**

**Testable before any third cortex exists — the teacher-plasticity titration:** partially unfreeze the word side of the *existing* loop and measure the teacher drift rate at which student conversion dies. That pins the cascading-stability constant empirically, from machinery already built. First post-loop experiment candidate; full standing discipline applies (own prereg, corridor, contrast-form bars).

## §3 The growing world (the curriculum law)

**The mechanism-level insight: growth starves rung 1.** In a fixed small world the marginal is eventually learnable *at the world level* — deck-average becomes a stable cheap solution again. In an expanding world the marginal is a perpetually stale moving target, while **object-level structure stays true across every expansion** — so the cheapest *stable* solution becomes the compositional one. This attacks the ladder's bottom rung in a way no within-dwell manipulation can.

**Formal shape:** nested environments E₁ ⊂ E₂ ⊂ …, with **stationarity of the old and expansion of the new** — the baby's rooms (home → house → yard → neighbourhood: those things don't change, but there is always more). "Reality is the teacher" refined: reality-as-granted-by-perception, scheduled. **Co-design with the gating law:** each expansion event is a distribution shift — exactly the plasticity-reopening trigger — so the world provides the surprise schedule and the gate consumes it. Design consequence for the substrate: the environment program should be built expansion-native (member pools, contexts, and axes that can grow without invalidating committed fabrics), which is cheap to honor now and expensive to retrofit.

## §5 Non-object concepts: the split, and the principle that lets time in

**Static relations** ("on," "bigger than," configurations) are co-present structure — in-frame, second-order co-occurrence, natively PAM-shaped. The missing prerequisite is concrete and mechanical: **multi-object waves** (today's fabric renders one member per wave). That is a substrate step, not a conceptual leap.

**Actions** are the true tension: temporal, and the founding fence bans forward prediction — the drift that killed ten restarts. The resolution: **the fence bans the arrow, not the extent.** Widen the wave — time-extended windows treated as one co-present structure, masked anywhere, completed **bidirectionally** from surrounding context. An action becomes a trajectory-shape *within* cortex-space — an object-in-time, recognized and completed, never forecast. **Time enters as extent, never as arrow.** Fence rider, explicit: this is the closest any banked direction comes to the forbidden move; its prereg requires a design-level fence review (Jason) before anything builds, and its anti-forward audit must show no loss term privileges later-time completion over earlier.

**Function words** ("and," "because," linkers) sit furthest out: they require the word channel to become a *stream* first, and cross-episode statistics after that. Banked honestly as emergent territory — no pretended path; the tell that PAM's structure is producing them would be completion behavior over word-word co-occurrence that no object grounds.

## §6 The vocabulary ladder (mirrors the reality ladder)

**objects → multi-object scenes (relations) → time-extended waves (actions) → word-as-stream (grammar/function words)** — each rung one substrate property, each with its fence note, each read against the rung below. The two ladders eventually meet: a growing world (§3) populated with multi-object, time-extended experience is the environment in which the upper vocabulary rungs are even posable.

## §7 Ordering and triggers

Everything here queues behind the conversion loop. First post-loop: the teacher-plasticity titration (§2) — smallest step, reuses everything, pins the constant multi-cortex needs. The growing-world program (§3) is the environment track and can be designed in parallel with late-ladder work. Extent-not-arrow (§5) waits for the fence review. Nothing in this document may be reached for as a rescue of the current loop — if the loop is stuck, the mechanism map governs, not the futures.

---

**Plain language.** After the learner can genuinely connect what it sees with what things are called, the road forks into three long projects. One: more minds — add a second and third cortex, with a strict seniority rule so students never teach students (we can measure how strict, today, by slowly unfreezing the current teacher until learning breaks). Two: a world that grows like a childhood — rooms that never change plus always one more door — because a world that keeps growing makes lazy averages permanently useless while making real understanding permanently worthwhile. Three: harder words — "on" and "bigger" arrive almost free once scenes hold two objects; verbs arrive only if we let time in as *duration to be understood* and never as *future to be predicted* (the one door this project must keep locked); and the little glue words come last, if they come, as an emergent surprise. None of this starts until the current question is answered — and none of it is allowed to rescue the current question.
