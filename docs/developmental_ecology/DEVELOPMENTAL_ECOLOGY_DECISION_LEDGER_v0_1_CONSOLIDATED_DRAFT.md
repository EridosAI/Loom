4. one ↔ several restorative surfaces;

10. one universal shape ↔ simple palette ↔ complex geometry;
11. homogeneous ↔ composite bodies;
12. anchored ↔ pushable furniture;
13. simple periodic mover ↔ complex movers ↔ other agents;
14. simulation ↔ physical embodiment;
15. weak association-to-cortex nudging ↔ stronger coupling;
16. spontaneous activity ↔ explicit intrinsic motivation;
17. immediate self-preservation ↔ longer-horizon optimisation and goals.

# F. Load-bearing open questions

These should remain individual high-attention forks rather than being hidden in a batch:

1. **Primitive cortex:** What operation allows each sensory channel to form useful changing representations without imposing mature objects or categories?
2. **Central association:** What is the minimal operation that can register recurrence, call forth related cortical states, and remain online from birth?
3. **Plasticity interface:** What association-to-cortex influence is permitted after EXP21, at what strength and timescale, and what keeps reality authoritative?
4. **Motor organisation:** How does accumulated association begin biasing motor activity without becoming a conventional policy optimiser?
5. **Drive interaction:** How do energy/integrity drive, current perception, spontaneous activity, and called-forth motor material combine without a hidden utility scalar?
6. **Phenomenon criterion:** What observation would justify saying that developmental ecology changed the regime rather than simply producing a competent reactive navigator?
