1. The memory _is_ a JEPA — cognition lives in the model, not in a separate predictor trained on a stream. (A replay store may feed it, but isn't the seat of association.)
2. Encoder gives similarity; memory gives association.
3. Memory's operation is a sideways query across all experience ("seen this? where?"), not forward-in-time prediction.
4. The trajectory is the thing being operated on — a density cloud through embedding space, fuzzy-edged and variable in extent — emergent from repeated use, not a stored object.
5. Patterns crystallise from repetition; instances decay in time — forgetting is a feature.
6. No phases — perceiving, learning, and remembering are one continuous operation.
7. No external objective — the system develops to keep the encoder–memory loop coherent, not to minimise a global loss.
8. Capacity grows with experience and stays dense — sized by history, grown by differentiation at the margins.
9. Multiple channels (vision, words, sound, touch) permanently co-stabilise each other; the association across them _is_ the internal concept (object, moment).
10. Encoder and memory co-evolve — the memory's prediction signal shapes what the encoder learns to notice.
11. The memory's purpose is to get better at the present moment
12. No plastic component without a slower-changing stable reference. Adaptive properties need stability.


Candidates:
- Time is felt not coded.
- Reality is the teacher, formation of the inner structure mirrors perception of the outer world. Complexity within enables perception of complexity without and vice versa.
  **No plastic component without a slower-changing reference.** Every component that learns must have something more stable than itself to converge against; that reference is itself on a relaxation schedule — stiff early when the system is chaotic, loosening as the rough configuration settles. Co-development loops without a stable reference do not converge. (Instances: the parent/word-stream stabilises the encoder; the fixed wave stabilises convergence learning; stiff routing stabilises cortex↔association translation. Each relaxes over development — parent recedes, wave becomes variable, routing becomes plastic.)
  
  - Every phase boundary is reference-and-relaxation: a slow reference stabilises a fast learner, then _becomes_ the reference for the next thing once it's settled.
  - Include the variable components from the beginning but fix the variable - unpin the variable once the system stabilises and let it out slowly. - Frozen Router, Pinned un-pooling gate and Relaxation Schedule.
