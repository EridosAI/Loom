## 1. The question and the design

**Does exogenous structured variation supply the differential fuel the closed loop
consumes — and can the teaching axis then discriminate what the static rig could not?**

**Arm grid: {no-detach, slowref, detachnull} × VARIED.** The static cells are the
EXISTING artifacts — no re-runs: no-detach-static = the entry run + word_terminal
(+ the s0 96k clock); slowref-static and detachnull-static = the EXP09 arms.

- **no-detach-varied is the direct test of the hypothesis: does exogenous variation
  alone hold the ORIGINAL loop open** — the deployed teaching topology, target-side
  pull intact, given an environment that keeps generating differences. **Pre-registered
  interpretation (verification-corrected — the licensed form):** if it does, CLOSURE
  was necessary too — the collapse was a property of the pull IN A CLOSED ENVIRONMENT,
  not of the pull alone; pull-necessity in the static regime (§10.12.1 DETACH —
  CONFIRMED) stays demonstrated either way.
- **slowref-varied vs detachnull-varied re-arms the §6 screen on the teaching axis:**
  under variation there is something to teach; a working reference and a bare
  amputation now have room to differ where the static rig gave them none.

## 2. THE UNPREDICTABILITY PIN (binding, three clauses — ruled 2026-07-04)

1. **Unpredictable as a sequence** — per-wave nuisance draws are stochastic; no cycles,
   no schedules. A deterministic rotation is just a larger closed loop and gets
   absorbed.
2. **Uninformative about identity** — nuisance ⟂ member ⟂ word ⟂ category, asserted
   NUMERICALLY at Step-0/manifest. Background that correlates with identity becomes a
   cue: spurious differential fuel, wrong-reason pass.
3. **Learnable as a distribution** — a fixed, recurring nuisance family (structured
   axes), so invariance is acquirable through exposure. Predictable at the family
   level, never at the instance level — that is what creates demand without re-closing
   the loop.
