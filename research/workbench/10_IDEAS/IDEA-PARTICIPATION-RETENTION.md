# Participation as a possible guide to retention

## What this proposes

P uses one learned support response for a faster change in sensory query participation and a slower reduction of coarsening. The proposed link is that consequential current participation may supply information before small structural retention effects become measurable. It does not establish that present usefulness equals long-term retention value.
