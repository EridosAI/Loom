A. PHYSICAL CEILING
--------------------------------------------------

Design bounded commissioning procedures to establish that the actual
body/world permits the necessary physical interactions.

At minimum cover:

- reaching energy sources;
- sustained productive energy contact;
- source depletion and later opportunity elsewhere;
- ordinary energy expenditure during travel;
- nonterminal mechanical damage;
- reaching restorative surfaces while damaged;
- maintaining repair-eligible gentle contact;
- actual positive integrity restoration;
- leaving repair with enough energy/capability to reach energy again;
- mover crossing, waiting or detour feasibility;
- physical accessibility under the actual body dimensions and collision
  laws.

A privileged commissioning controller may use simulator state here,
because it is an apparatus test, not part of the organism.

Do not convert that controller into an organism component.

For every proposed physical check state:
- what it proves;
- what it does NOT prove;
- exact privileged information used;
- whether it changes the world trajectory;
- proposed finite horizon;
- possible apparatus-failure interpretations.

Do not invent arbitrary success percentiles merely to obtain gates.

- source approach/contact opportunities;
- source state changes where physically represented;
- restorative-surface interaction;
- mover approach/motion;
- body-wall/contact relation;

Do not install object IDs, global position, analytic gradients, material

- information absent from sensors;

- information available to the cortex but lost in its wave packet.

--------------------------------------------------
C. BOOTSTRAP FLOOR
--------------------------------------------------

Design a bounded assessment of the fixed newborn spontaneous process.

The question is NOT whether random/spontaneous activity solves the world.

The question is whether it sometimes provides the first consequential
experiences from which development could proceed.

Design measures for:

- first source encounter;
- useful source contact;
- first mechanical stress/damage encounter;
- restorative-surface contact;
- useful repair while damaged;
- exposure to mover dynamics;
- continuing experience after the first useful interaction.

Include deaths/nonencounters rather than conditioning on survivors.

Also examine the opposite failure:
the innate process should not by itself maintain the organism indefinitely
in a way that leaves little developmental problem to solve.

Do not set a developmental-success gate during commissioning.
