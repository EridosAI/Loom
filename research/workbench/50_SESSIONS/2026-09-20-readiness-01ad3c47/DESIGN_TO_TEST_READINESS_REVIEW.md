## 2. Scientific questions and limits of success

### P: does consequential present participation help preserve useful distinctions?

A faithful P could investigate whether experience-shaped, context-sensitive **group participation** can both alter current responses and support useful lasting sensory differentiation across recurring encounters. Its fast route changes query input at the next handoff; its slower route reduces coarsening of fine parameters. The hypothesis is that the shorter consequence route can help learn support before small retention effects become behaviourally measurable. This is an untested proxy relationship, not an identity between current emphasis and long-term value. [P §0 and §6](../../90_SOURCES/candidate_comparison_2026-09-20/sources/participation_retention/LOOM_ASSOCIATIVE_REGULATORY_CANDIDATE_v0_1_DISCUSSION_DRAFT.md#6-the-sensory-participationretention-link); [P §9](../../90_SOURCES/candidate_comparison_2026-09-20/sources/participation_retention/LOOM_ASSOCIATIVE_REGULATORY_CANDIDATE_v0_1_DISCUSSION_DRAFT.md#9-one-handoff-and-one-subsequent-interval); [Comparison §§4–5.1](../../90_SOURCES/candidate_comparison_2026-09-20/REVIEW_AND_RECONCILIATION_NOTES.md#4-the-useful-new-hypothesis-expression-can-precede-structural-benefit).

There are three separable possible findings: useful immediate regulation; persistent associative/regulatory response change; and useful persistent sensory change to which retention support contributes. A result establishing the first does not establish the others. A negative result in which motor regulation learns but sensory support does not can still discriminate a weakness of the proposed link.

Even a convincing positive under one specified coupling would **not** establish that:

- fine differences caused the useful group effect: P's gain changes common activity as well as differences, so a group can earn support even when its differential activity contributes nothing;
- current emphasis is generally a correct estimate of long-term retention value, or query floors guarantee correction;
- improved navigation or bodily condition came from sensory development rather than predominantly regulatory weights, innate dynamics or the supplied adapter;
- association is a full joint model, recall reconstructs whole episodes, or finite traces solve unrestricted delayed credit;
- bodily preference or a desire to live emerged unaided, the law is reward-free, or the regulator/scaffold can later be removed.

Those are explicit source limitations or consequences of the comparison, not reasons to discard P. [Comparison §5.2, lines 71–81](../../90_SOURCES/candidate_comparison_2026-09-20/REVIEW_AND_RECONCILIATION_NOTES.md#52-a-useful-group-need-not-owe-its-usefulness-to-its-fine-differences); [P §§8, 11–12](../../90_SOURCES/candidate_comparison_2026-09-20/sources/participation_retention/LOOM_ASSOCIATIVE_REGULATORY_CANDIDATE_v0_1_DISCUSSION_DRAFT.md#11-analytical-checks-and-admitted-limitations).

### R: can consequence-shaped support preserve sensory structure without direct query-gain control?

A faithful R could investigate whether sensory-local provisional distinctions, contextual association and learned regulation can produce useful changes on recurrence, with reduced coarsening making a demonstrable contribution to lasting sensory organisation. Regulation changes a specified persistence force rather than supplying a remembered sensory target. A weaker but distinguishable possible finding is useful associative/regulatory adaptation without evidence that sensory retention helped. [R §0 and §7.3](../../90_SOURCES/candidate_comparison_2026-09-20/sources/retention_only/LOOM_ASSOCIATIVE_REGULATION_WORKED_CANDIDATE_v0_1.md#73-hold-against-coarsening); [R §10](../../90_SOURCES/candidate_comparison_2026-09-20/sources/retention_only/LOOM_ASSOCIATIVE_REGULATION_WORKED_CANDIDATE_v0_1.md#10-one-chronological-encounter-without-a-next-state-model).

“Retention-only” describes R's **direct sensory return**. R still has fast motor/regulatory effects, and actual regulatory activity enters association. A support perturbation may therefore influence subsequent behaviour through regulatory correlations without teaching the value of the slowly preserved sensory structure. If that slower benefit falls beyond the informative consequence bridge, this challenges the realization; it does not show the lost distinction was useless. [R §§6, 9.3](../../90_SOURCES/candidate_comparison_2026-09-20/sources/retention_only/LOOM_ASSOCIATIVE_REGULATION_WORKED_CANDIDATE_v0_1.md#93-a-particularly-weak-link-sensory-support-credit); [Comparison §4](../../90_SOURCES/candidate_comparison_2026-09-20/REVIEW_AND_RECONCILIATION_NOTES.md#4-the-useful-new-hypothesis-expression-can-precede-structural-benefit).

Success would not by itself establish useful sensory development, correct causal credit, emergent bodily preference, long-range episodic recall or removal of the regulator. Smaller coarsening terms or larger retained coefficients are not sufficient: a retained nuisance can also produce those records, and a changed coefficient does not guarantee a useful functional distinction. An injury-related association remaining available while an action is restrained would be different from simply forgetting that association. [R §§9.2, 11–12](../../90_SOURCES/candidate_comparison_2026-09-20/sources/retention_only/LOOM_ASSOCIATIVE_REGULATION_WORKED_CANDIDATE_v0_1.md#11-analytic-checks-and-limits-before-any-experiment); [Direction §§8.2–8.3](../../90_SOURCES/direction_2026-09-18/LOOM_DEVELOPMENTAL_DIRECTION_v0_1_REVIEW_DRAFT.md#82-recipient-specific-consequences).

### Common claim ceiling

The defensible ambition is a **candidate-specific, finite, history-dependent change in organisation under disclosed innate capacities and bodily preference**, with each claimed contribution supported separately. No finite positive demonstrates indefinite sustainability, general intelligence, consciousness, subjective desire, mature planning or a general world model. No negative automatically refutes every developmental-grounding account. Apparatus failure, a specified mechanism's inadequacy and a mismatch between their timescales remain distinguishable possibilities. [Design Frame §§9, 21–22](../../90_SOURCES/reference_7ada2b300fa1/docs/developmental_ecology/DEVELOPMENTAL_ECOLOGY_DESIGN_FRAME_v0_2.md#22-claim-ceiling); [Base World §§8.2, 13](../../90_SOURCES/reference_7ada2b300fa1/docs/developmental_ecology/BASE_WORLD_COMPLETION_v0_1.md#82-interpretation-protections); [Direction §§1, 10.2, 12](../../90_SOURCES/direction_2026-09-18/LOOM_DEVELOPMENTAL_DIRECTION_v0_1_REVIEW_DRAFT.md#102-conventional-driver-proposal-to-evaluate-not-acceptance).

### C. Empirical uncertainties that should remain investigable

The following should not block forming an explicit candidate merely because their answer may be negative:

- Whether the sensory-local law discovers useful weak distinctions rather than nuisance variance or redundant rows.
- Whether finite packets, traces and contextual pairwise statistics support the needed recurrence-sensitive associations, despite aliasing and changing cortical coordinates.
- Whether perturbation/consequence learning obtains useful credit, especially for costly travel, avoided damage and retention with delayed benefit.
- Whether current group support is a useful guide to retaining fine structure, including rarely useful distinctions.
- Whether two bodily banks cooperate sufficiently, rather than produce cancellation, excessive restraint or energy-saving immobility.
- Whether references, map-use preservation and regulatory recurrence stabilize useful organisation or preserve errors and self-confirming habits.
- Whether a complete coupled organism develops something attributable to sensory/associative change rather than mostly adapter competence.

The distinction is **an operation must be specified; its success need not be presumed**. Undefined coordinates, a disconnected pathway or inaccessible required information are design/apparatus problems. Failure of an accessible, defined mechanism to learn is a possible scientific result. Increasing a trace is a proposed parameter choice, not proof of correct credit. Repeated failure to learn does not establish that a distinction was unimportant. [P §11](../../90_SOURCES/candidate_comparison_2026-09-20/sources/participation_retention/LOOM_ASSOCIATIVE_REGULATORY_CANDIDATE_v0_1_DISCUSSION_DRAFT.md#11-analytical-checks-and-admitted-limitations); [R §§9.3,12](../../90_SOURCES/candidate_comparison_2026-09-20/sources/retention_only/LOOM_ASSOCIATIVE_REGULATION_WORKED_CANDIDATE_v0_1.md#12-main-possible-reasons-this-candidate-is-wrong); [Direction §§9,12,15](../../90_SOURCES/direction_2026-09-18/LOOM_DEVELOPMENTAL_DIRECTION_v0_1_REVIEW_DRAFT.md#9-provisional-distinction-use-and-recurrent-pooling-cycles).

## 5. What an unchanged P–R comparison would compare

It would compare bundled whole-organism accounts. Even matched world settings, seeds or parameter counts would not remove the following mechanism differences. Once action diverges, the experienced inputs also diverge.

| Dimension | P as written | R as written |
|---|---|---|
| Receptor input and native dynamics | Receptor-mean subtraction; fixed generally asymmetric recurrent matrix; local coactivity competition in learning. | Receptor-level drive; common-mode activity competition and a lag state; normalized local Hebbian content update. |
| Temporal packet | Mean and endpoint of recurrent activity, plus central fading context. | Mean, second moments and signed activity–lag pair interactions, plus central context. |
| Context gate inputs | Receiving-channel gates exclude receiver but can include source. | Shared pair gates exclude both channels in the pair. |
| Associative maps and preservation | Independently learned directed maps; retrieval-use-dependent decay. | Reciprocal transpose-constrained maps; constant decay. |

| Bodily interaction | Summed/logit contributions can offset; separate current contributions and brake. | Either bank can protect a difference; permissions multiply; signed current contributions also act. |

### 6.4 What distinguishes immediate regulation from lasting development?

**First, partition the explanation.** A fast output can come from long-learned regulatory parameters; a long-lasting behavioural change can come from Theta or H without useful change in W. “Immediate effect” and “short-lived learning” are not synonyms. Track transient activity/context/eligibility, lasting cortical/associative/regulatory structure and slow references separately, together with actual world history.

**Second, observe function as well as magnitude.** Changes in W, pooling occupancy or coarsening are structural evidence, not yet useful sensory change. Functional readouts need a declared sensory/history context and must account for changing coordinate systems, bodily state and current regulation. In P, stronger h can reflect a useful common signal; in R, reduced damping can protect a useless coefficient. Continued improvement after an encounter can also reflect lingering traces or a different physical opportunity.

**Third, preserve the ability to ask causal questions later.** Passive plots can show timing and association, not identify the contribution of sensory development. A later authorized assessment could use preserved state copies or separately specified lives to distinguish (a) acute dependence on current return after development, (b) dependence on that pathway while structure formed, and (c) functional effects of persistent sensory structure versus H/Theta. These answer different questions. There is no ablation matrix, selected intervention, scheduled trial or verdict rule here; the recommendation is to retain the state partitions and records that make a limited later assessment possible.

Such assessments must not mutate the original life or become learner replay/training data. Replaying a recorded receptor sequence on a preserved analysis copy, if later authorized, is an observer diagnostic rather than an episode store inside the organism. Arbitrarily clearing context or eligibility may create an off-regime state; matching or initialization rules would have to be explicit. Persistence after one convenient reset does not by itself establish development.

This preserves synthesis before reduction: form and observe the complete coupling first, then ask focused attribution questions about any functioning phenomenon. It also preserves the difference between reliably re-evoking regulation and transferring its function elsewhere.
