# P commissioning configuration-change rules v0.1

**Status:** assistant proposal; no adjustment authorized. Applies to `6bc9683b54e4fa80136fe8534d7713e2a250a95f`. Read with [the design](P_COUPLING_COMMISSIONING_DESIGN_v0_1_REVIEW_DRAFT.md) and [the exact configuration](REFERENCES/developmental_ecology/configuration.json).

## Authority and dependency notation

Every top-level configuration field is assigned below, including metadata. Grouping fields does not authorize changing them together. Every proposal must name the actual field, old/new value, exact defect, discriminating observation, alternatives and affected records. No automatic range or adjustment route is approved. Jason authorizes every trajectory-affecting adjustment. An implementer can diagnose and propose; authorization to execute this design would not alone authorize numerical changes.

**V** = identity, schema, arithmetic, records and observer non-interference. **P** = affected physical/accounting/geometry witnesses. **S** = raw sensory contrasts, information boundaries and prehistory. **B** = unconditional newborn opportunity at the unchanged declared seed schedule. **O** = cortical/packet/association/credit/receiver observability. A rerun dependency means relevant prior checks lose coverage; it does not grant permission to rerun. Preserve every previous configuration and result. Confirm apparently unaffected dependencies explicitly.

Evidence of a **configuration defect** must concern whether the scoped question can be posed, not whether the answer looks promising. For a mechanism-defined setting, a demonstrated limitation can require Jason to narrow the question or authorize a new branch; it need not be a fixable instrument defect. Adjusting a value to conceal an implementation-law mismatch is forbidden.

## Freeze consequence

Before freeze these are conservative proposal rules, not a standing license to calibrate. After the explicit complete-coupling freeze described in the main design, any trajectory-affecting or interpretation-changing value gets a new configuration/branch identity. AV defects can invalidate their affected evidence; poor SO cannot. No data is erased, no old checkpoint is retrospectively “fixed,” and no commissioning life becomes an evidential trial by relabelling it.
