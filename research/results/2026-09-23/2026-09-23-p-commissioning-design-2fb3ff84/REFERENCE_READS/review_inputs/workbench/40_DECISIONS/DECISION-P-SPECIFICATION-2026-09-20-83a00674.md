## Accepted scope

| Decision | Accepted effect | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | Carry useful history-dependent regulation and useful lasting sensory change as separate questions in the complete organism's specification. Preserve records and later assessment capability needed to distinguish immediate effects, H/Theta learning and sensory change. | Survival/navigation improvement or changed coefficients alone do not establish sensory contribution. No ablation campaign or scientific success gate selected. |
| D2 | Permit P's specified separate perturbation/consequence scaffold to update corresponding regulatory banks from actual E/I, their filters and declared internal participation/eligibility. Retain the reference, bound and update order. | Candidate-scoped exception, not programme-wide permission for policy optimisation or extra helpful controllers. No combined reward/value/critic, trajectory optimiser, semantic action selector, privileged source/outcome input or direct bodily training of W/H. |
| D3 | Specify intact P v0.1 first, using the exact parent identity below. Preserve R and all alternative families. | No P–R synthesis, no rejection of R, no compulsory control, no universal validation of P's mechanisms. |
| Walkthrough | Provide readable continuing-loop, exact-handoff and recurrence views, two hypothetical encounter traces and a signal/equation/record crosswalk. | Prose must match specified arithmetic and timing, expose conventional mechanisms, and distinguish actual consequence from evocation. |

D2 retains P's enumerated sensory support, signed motor currents and motor attenuation; each bodily bank can affect every output type. P's logit/current superposition is supplied anatomy. Bodily scores do not directly train sensory weights, supply their targets or decide which encounters H records. The accepted claim is development under a disclosed bodily preference and learning asymmetry, not emergence of preference or a guarantee that the scaffold can later be removed. Removal or replacement remains a later explicit decision.
