# 1. Sequence ruling

**[JASON-ACCEPTED WORKING]**

The immediate Loom sequence changes after EXP21.

The previously reconstructed next permitted object was a design ruling about PAM–cortex representation ownership and what PAM is permitted to change. The new direction does **not** discard that problem, but it does not proceed directly into repairing the isolated teaching pathway.

Instead:

> **Step outward and test whether part of the missing stabilising condition is supplied by the organism’s continuing relationship with a sufficiently rich reality.**

This is a real sequence change, not a side experiment.

EXP21 remains valid. It showed that unrestricted direct PAM pressure produced category-aligned compression or no effect on its bench. The developmental-ecology line asks whether that bench removed conditions under which association, sensory development, action, consequence, and reality would normally constrain one another.

The PAM–cortex interface question is therefore **banked inside the wider developmental problem**, not silently resolved.

---

# 2. Purpose of the new line

**[JASON-ACCEPTED WORKING]**

The aim is not to build mature faculties one at a time and then integrate them. It is to construct the smallest continuing organism–world relationship in which development can genuinely occur.

Minimality means:

> **minimum imposed ontology, not minimum number of interacting variables.**

A primitive life may require several crude capacities simultaneously:

- a persistent body;
- several partial sensory windows;
- the ability to act;
- plastic and persistent internal state;
- association;
- bodily consequence;
- a lawful world with its own continuity.

Each capacity can be extremely poor by mature standards. Their interaction, not the sophistication of any single component, is the object of study.

---

# 3. Grounding principle: reality is the teacher

**[CANON-INHERITED]**

> **Reality is the teacher.**

The system is not to be trained toward an externally specified representation of reality. Environment supplies experience; experience changes structure; changed structure alters subsequent perception and response; the loop continues.

Existing Loom doctrine already includes:

- no train/run phase separation;
- multiple channels that can co-stabilise one another;
- time felt through dynamics rather than supplied as an index;
- concepts arising through association rather than being installed as objects;
- coherence learned rather than imposed.

The developmental-ecology line treats those commitments as properties of a **life**, not merely properties of a learning loss.

---

# 4. Reality supplies causal coherence before the organism represents it

**[JASON-ACCEPTED WORKING]**

A phenomenon does not become coherent because an internal mechanism binds together several unrelated modality targets.

A tree exists. Its geometry affects light. Its material resists contact. Wind acting on it changes its movement and sound. Walking around it changes viewpoint and occlusion. Those consequences belong together because they arise from one persistent cause.

Therefore:

> **The world does not need to teach the organism that several sensory experiences belong together. Their belonging together is already a consequence of what exists.**

A simulated world should reproduce this relation at the organism’s scale:

> **one persistent world state → several lawful sensory and action-dependent consequences.**

It should not reproduce it through separately authored modality labels or matching codes.

---

# 5. Associative consistency, not imposed binding

**[PROPOSED]**

The developmental task may be better described as **associative consistency** than as internal binding.

> **Repeated experience should cause the organism’s internal relations to become increasingly consistent with recurring causal relations in reality.**

This does not require all sensory spaces to collapse into one point or one common latent. Loom’s own-space cortex principle remains live. The consistency could eventually be realised through reciprocal mappings, distributed basins, associative trajectories, a separate association space, or another mechanism not yet conceived.

The design frame intentionally does not select the implementation.

---

# 6. Developmental critical mass

**[PROPOSED]**

There may be a qualitative threshold in organism–reality coupling.

> **A persistent embodied associative system may enter a different developmental regime once its relationship with reality contains sufficient causal richness, sensory diversity, recurrence, agency, and consequence for many parts of its internal structure to become mutually conditioned by experience.**

The threshold may not primarily be model size or parameter count. It may concern the density of mutually relevant relationships available to one continuing organism.

Below the threshold, locally cheap organisations may remain adequate.

Above it, a single destructive compression may fail because the same internal structure is repeatedly needed in many different relationships.

Whether such a threshold exists is an empirical question. It must not be assumed from philosophical elegance.

---

# 7. Actual reality and simulated reality are not equivalent design problems

**[JASON-ACCEPTED WORKING]**

Actual physical reality is multiscale and effectively inexhaustible relative to biological organisms. A slug and an eagle inhabit the same physical reality while accessing radically different parts of its structure through different bodies and perceptual windows.

A finite simulation contains only the scales, laws, causal depth, and affordances that we put into it.

Therefore:

> **“The world must contain affordances at the organism’s scale” is primarily a simulation-validity rule, not a general claim about reality.**

A computationally complicated world may still be developmentally empty for a particular body. The ant in a world whose meaningful dynamics occur only at tonne-boulder scale has little effective agency, regardless of the simulator’s complexity.

Physical realism is optional. **Causal fidelity at the organism’s perceptual and action scale is not.**

---

# 8. The organism–world coupling is the experimental object

**[JASON-ACCEPTED WORKING]**

The organism is not studied while treating the environment as neutral background. The environment is not studied independently of the body capable of accessing it.

The developmental object is their coupling:

> **reality × body × sensory window × agency × viability × time.**

The same external world can support different internal organisations depending on:

- modality and receptor placement;
- sensory range and resolution;
- sampling rate;
- body scale and strength;
- available actions;
- memory and plasticity timescales;
- internal bodily state;
- which environmental changes the organism can actually cause.

The form of whatever eventually develops may be a historical consequence of this coupling rather than a universal canonical world representation.

---

# 9. Coupling adequacy and developmental reachability

**[JASON-ACCEPTED WORKING]**

A successful behaviour existing somewhere in the world is not sufficient.

> **A viable outcome must be developmentally reachable, not merely physically possible.**

There must be a continuous path from the organism’s innate activity to increasingly effective interaction, where early steps produce consequences the organism can sense, associate, survive, and act upon at its current level of development.

The initial ecology should therefore provide:

- at least one birth-accessible foothold;
- graded rather than all-or-nothing consequences;
- repeated opportunities;
- temporal gaps within the system’s usable associative reach;
- enough reserve and robustness for early failure;
- room for increasingly organised behaviour to outperform spontaneous activity.

A developmental null may arise from:

1. an inadequate organism;
2. an inadequate world;
3. a mismatch between them.

No null is attributed automatically to the learner before the coupling itself has been commissioned as a valid instrument.

---

# 10. Designed worlds should contain laws, not lessons

**[JASON-ACCEPTED WORKING]**

> **Design causes and interaction laws; do not design training examples.**

The world should not schedule lessons such as “now learn colour” or “now contrast these objects.” Experience should arise because a persistent organism lives under fixed laws.

A useful diagnostic question is:

> **Would this event still occur under the world’s laws if no learner were present whose development we were trying to help?**

If not, the event may be curriculum rather than world.

The base world must also not be adversarially decorrelated merely to eliminate easy associations. Stable signatures and repeated causes are allowed and likely necessary.

> **Consistent cause, variable manifestation.**

A cause may remain recognisably itself while its sensory expression changes lawfully with distance, orientation, occlusion, diffusion, depletion, contact, and physical state.

Reality is allowed to be learnable.

---

# 11. Hidden simulator knowledge is not organism knowledge

**[JASON-ACCEPTED WORKING]**

The simulator may know:

- persistent-body identity;
- position and geometry;
- material parameters;
- resource stock;
- causal history;
- energy and integrity state;
- contact and collision details.

That privileged state is available for external measurement and apparatus validation.

It must not enter the organism as:

- IDs or labels;
- semantic categories;
- global coordinates;
- object masks;
- attention targets;
- direction-to-source vectors;
- source-stock values;
- reward or utility;
- explicit cross-modal correspondence.

The organism receives only what its body can encounter.

---

# 12. One persistent world and one persistent organism

**[JASON-ACCEPTED WORKING; CANON-ALIGNED]**

There must be a continuing world rather than a sequence of samples.

There must also be one continuing organism.

> **The organism encountering the next moment is the changed organism that experienced the previous ones.**

Its history need not be stored as an explicit archive. History can exist because having lived changes the structure now encountering the present.

The primitive experimental unit is therefore not a training example or episode.

It is closer to:

> **a life.**

The world itself may have a lawful history before that life begins. Pre-evolving a chemical field, for example, is not organism pretraining; it prevents organism age from being encoded by an artificial zero-state startup.

---

# 13. Agency begins before purpose

**[JASON-ACCEPTED WORKING]**

The organism must be able to act in ways that change its subsequent relation to the world.

Purposeful planning is not required at birth.

The accepted initial branch supplies **endogenous, bounded, temporally structured spontaneous activity**. This moves the organism before it understands movement and prevents immediate quiescence.

It does not settle:

- what ultimately acts;
- why activity exists;
- how decisive action emerges;
- whether curiosity emerges;
- how inspired or genuinely generative action develops.

The honest claim boundary is:

> **Spontaneous action is innate; the organisation of action may develop.**

Possible later phenomena remain distinct:

- decisive action — one tendency becomes selectively stabilised in context;
- curious action — unresolved but learnable situations increasingly call forth exploration;
- inspired action — experience is recombined into action not reducible to direct repetition.

No explicit novelty reward, surprise maximisation, or belief that “trying will improve things” is supplied at birth.

---

# 14. Consequence, viability, and primitive valence

**[JASON-ACCEPTED WORKING]**

A consequence-free organism–world loop may be insufficient for meaningful action.

The first complete organism therefore contains two bodily viability dimensions:

- **energy** — current capacity to perform ongoing work;
- **integrity** — continuing physical coherence and reliability of the body.

They are separate causal currencies. They are not aggregated into reward, health, utility, fitness, or a hidden scalar action-selection signal.

However, they are not neutral telemetry.

> **Higher energy and greater integrity are intrinsically preferable embodied conditions within their viable ranges. Depletion produces dimension-specific interoceptive drive and lawful capability loss; restoration reduces that drive and improves available functioning.**

This supplies primitive valence and self-preservative need.

It does **not** supply knowledge of:

- what in the world restores either dimension;
- where it is;
- how to reach it;
- which action is appropriate;
- how to trade the two needs across time.

The design gives the organism the need, not the strategy.

Viability dimensionality remains a dial: one dimension is a later reduction; three or more are later expansions.

---

# 15. What may be present at birth

**[PROPOSED FRAME, ACCEPTED IN DIRECTION]**

Innate structure may include capacities required for experience:

- a persistent body;
- sensory transduction;
- body-relative actuation;
- persistent internal state;
- some form of plasticity;
- an associative operation;
- enough stabilising dynamics for change to accumulate;
- spontaneous activity;
- interoceptive access to viability;
- primitive valence;
- whatever rhythms or sampling structure are genuinely necessary.

These are capacities, not knowledge of what the world contains.

---

# 16. What should not be present at birth if emergence is the claim

**[PROPOSED GUARD, ACCEPTED IN DIRECTION]**

Do not provide mature abstractions merely because mature intelligence possesses them:

- object identities;
- semantic categories;
- explicit concepts;
- predefined attention targets;
- salience labels;
- episodic boundaries;
- a self token or explicit self-model;
- a privileged world state;
- semantic rewards;
- a prebuilt planning objective;
- long-horizon goals;
- explicit multimodal binding;
- a requirement to construct a conventional next-state world model.

If a later branch introduces one of these innately, the corresponding phenomenon is no longer claimed as emergent without further qualification.

---

# 17. Plasticity is reopened

**[JASON-ACCEPTED WORKING]**

EXP21 must not be converted into the assumption that PAM simply needs a stronger or more sophisticated way to teach cortical representations.

The deeper question is reopened:

> **If persistent external causes already bind sensory consequences, how much and what kind of associative influence must enter sensory cortices at all?**

Possible answers range from substantial co-development to very weak nudging.

The criterion is no longer:

> how quickly can PAM make vision acquire the named category?

It is:

> **What plastic relation allows the organism to become increasingly consistent with experience without any internal component gaining unilateral authority over reality?**

No answer is selected by this frame.

---

# 18. Stability may be relational

**[PROPOSED]**

Some stability may arise because many recurring relationships continue to agree on parts of the same internal structure.

A representation may remain stable not because a global stabiliser opposes plasticity, but because changing it would make many useful associations inconsistent with future experience.

Where experience stops supporting a distinction, that overdetermination may weaken and allow reorganisation or coarsening.

This does not supersede pooling-state capacity, reference-and-relaxation, or other accepted mechanisms. It changes their interpretation from presumed sole sources of stability to candidate local machinery operating inside a wider ecological source of consistency.

---

# 19. Synthesis before reduction

**[JASON-ACCEPTED WORKING]**

> **First construct the smallest defensible complete organism–world relationship and look for a developmental phenomenon. Only after the phenomenon exists should reduction determine what was necessary.**

This reverses the recent experimental order, not the commitment to rigor.

Nature did not isolate memory, vision, action, and viability before permitting a primitive organism to exist. Scientific reduction is most informative once there is a functioning phenomenon to remove parts from.

The first synthesis remains tightly controlled:

- world laws frozen;
- organism laws frozen;
- innate structure enumerated;
- privileged information enumerated and blocked;
- external probes fixed and trajectory-inert;
- wrong-reason outcomes pre-named;
- no mid-life repair;
- multiple lifetimes/seeds;
- full developmental trajectories retained.

---

# 20. Commissioning is not outcome rescue

**[JASON-ACCEPTED WORKING]**

The first runs will likely include **coupling-commissioning lifetimes**.

Legitimate commissioning asks whether the apparatus poses the developmental question at all:

- can the organism physically reach a source?
- can its actual senses distinguish relevant states?
- can spontaneous activity sometimes reach the first rung?
- are consequences graded and survivable?
- are timescales within associative reach?

If not, the apparatus is revised, the change is recorded, and no evidential claim is made.

Once the coupling passes its validity checks and an evidential cohort opens, changing source placement, sensor range, block speed, damage magnitude, or other numerical geometry to obtain a desired developmental outcome is not commissioning. It is a new branch.

The standing rule remains:

> **Report; do not patch.**

---

# 21. Developmental observation rather than task performance

**[PROPOSED]**

The first line should not be judged primarily by navigation score, classification accuracy, or a scalar reward.

External observations may ask whether:

- recurring causes become mutually associative across channels;
- partial perception calls forth consequences not currently present;
- spontaneous action becomes state-sensitive and selectively organised;
- different bodily deficits condition different behaviour;
- familiar causes remain identifiable across lawful manifestation change;
- source identity and current source state begin to separate;
- old useful distinctions survive new associations;
- action–consequence structure becomes increasingly legible internally;
- the organism behaves differently toward self-contingent and world-autonomous change;
- environmental manipulation, once introduced, leaves behaviourally useful external traces.

These are researcher observations. They do not backpropagate or enter the organism as objectives.

---

# 22. Claim ceiling

**[PROPOSED]**

Even a striking first run would not demonstrate intelligence, consciousness, a general world model, planning, or a theory of mind.

The strongest early claim should be closer to:

> **Under a specified organism–world coupling, ongoing associative and plastic dynamics generated internally measurable structure and selectively organised behaviour not supplied as training targets, and those changes reflected recurring causal relations in the organism’s experience.**

Failure would not by itself refute the grounding philosophy. It would show that the particular organism, world, or coupling did not instantiate sufficient conditions. The programme must progressively distinguish those alternatives rather than use that fact as an unfalsifiable escape.

---

# 23. Physical reality remains an escalation

**[JASON-ACCEPTED WORKING]**

The initial habitat is a continuous generative causal simulation.

Physical embodiment remains a standing escalation if uncertainty about the designed world becomes dominant.

Actual reality has already supplied multiscale causal richness, niches, and affordances without enumerating them for us. At some point a small embodied platform may become the cleaner experiment—not because robotics is inherently superior, but because physical reality provides a control against our assumptions about what a sufficient simulated reality must contain.

---

# 24. Terms deliberately left unsettled

**[OPEN]**

Do not yet canonise **ecological closure** as the name of the target phenomenon.

Related but distinct ideas remain:

- **sensorimotor closure** — sensing affects action and action changes subsequent sensing;
- **causal coherence** — multiple consequences arise from persistent shared causes;
- **coupling adequacy** — the body can access and affect meaningful parts of its world;
- **developmental reachability** — an immature organism can reach the first learnable rung;
- **developmental sufficiency** — the complete relation is rich enough for the phenomenon under study;
- **viability** — bodily states affect continuing capacity to exist and act;
- **associative consistency** — internal relations become increasingly consistent with recurring world relations.

The experimental line should tell us which of these becomes load-bearing.

---

# 25. Explicitly unresolved mechanism questions

**[OPEN]**

This frame does not settle:

1. the primitive cortical operation inside each sensory channel;
2. the central associative mechanism;
3. the amount and route of association-to-cortex plastic influence;
4. whether current pooling-state capacity is sufficient;
5. how spontaneous activity is implemented;
6. how interoceptive drive, current perception, called-forth motor material, and competing needs combine;
7. how decisive, curious, or inspired action develops;
8. whether a separate memory store is required;
9. the correct wave/rate relationship across modalities;
10. the minimum developmental result that distinguishes regime change from competent navigation;
11. when simulation uncertainty should trigger physical embodiment.

These are future forks, not omissions to fill silently.

---

# 26. One-line spine

> **Build the smallest continuing organism capable of sensing and acting within a causally coherent world; provide capacities for association, plasticity, persistence, and primitive viability, but as little ontology as possible; then let its history with reality determine what internal structure and organised action become necessary.**

Companion line:

> **Reality does not need to tell the organism which sensory experiences belong together. Their belonging together is already a consequence of what exists.**
