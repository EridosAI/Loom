# P commissioning: a plain-language walkthrough v0.1

**A proposed path for review, not a report of a run.** The organism is the independently reviewed P checkpoint `6bc9683b54e4fa80136fe8534d7713e2a250a95f`. It is fit to proceed to coupling commissioning and remains **uncommissioned / untested**. Nothing described here has been executed in this contribution.

The question is: **How do we know this world gives this newborn a fair developmental opportunity without tuning it toward success?** We need several different kinds of evidence. A reachable source is not necessarily detectable. A detectable source is not necessarily represented by P's cortex. A represented encounter is not necessarily remembered usefully. Keeping those differences visible prevents us from changing the world every time the organism disappoints us.

The [full design](P_COUPLING_COMMISSIONING_DESIGN_v0_1_REVIEW_DRAFT.md), [matrix](P_COMMISSIONING_MATRIX_v0_1_REVIEW_DRAFT.md) and [decision list](DECISIONS_REQUIRED_BEFORE_EXECUTION.md) give the exact proposed procedures, limits and outstanding authority.

## First, demonstrate what a body can physically do

Imagine starting with an external operator who can see the body's position, the sources, the moving block, the contact forces, energy and integrity. This operator is a testing instrument. It is not part of P, and its information is never installed in the organism.

The operator has only the actual body's two actuators. It cannot teleport, add energy, erase damage, replenish a source or change a collision. If the body is too wide to pass a gap, an attractive line on a point-agent map does not count as a route. We first check the geometry using the real body radius, then demonstrate actual movement and its costs.

Suppose the operator reaches a source. We would inspect three different things: contact occurred; stock really moved from the source into the body; and, during a stated interval, intake exceeded expenditure so the body's energy actually increased. A touch without transfer is not useful energy contact. Transfer that remains below expenditure is still real transfer, but not a net gain.

Next the operator stays long enough to draw the stock down, leaves and reaches another source. The first source continues to renew; “depleted” must not mean that its stock is required to reach exactly zero. The world continues throughout. There is no reset after contact and no fresh world when the body departs.

The configured numbers already tell us something limited but useful. One source can renew at no more than 0.0005 energy units per second, while the body spends at least 0.0015 per second. One finite source cannot support indefinite camping. All eight sources together have a larger theoretical renewal capacity, but that does not establish that a real body can collect enough while travelling and repairing. A proposed longer controlled circuit would measure those actual costs and stock changes. Even a successful 1,200-second circuit would remain finite evidence.

Now consider damage and repair. One test would produce real, nonterminal injury, then steer the damaged body to a restorative wall. A separate manufactured low-energy, low-integrity starting state would inspect a harder recovery condition. That second fixture would be labelled as manufactured; it would not masquerade as an ordinary newborn history.

Repair requires the right kind of physical contact. Pressing harder is not always better. The operator would try a gentle force inside the permitted range, inspect actual positive integrity restoration, leave before expenditure consumes the remaining energy, and reach energy again. We would not require complete integrity: under the implemented law, perfect restoration is approached gradually. The important physical witness is real repair followed by a feasible return to energy.

Finally, the operator would demonstrate crossing, waiting and detouring around the mover. The whole swept region is not permanently forbidden; timing matters. These witnesses would establish possibilities under specified conditions. They would not establish that P can see them, discover them, learn them or execute them reliably. A failed operator attempt would also not automatically establish impossibility: poor control must be distinguished from an invalid body/world relationship.

## Then remove the privileged view

The next proposed controller has no world map or object names. The simplest first version is a human looking only at the organism's actual sensory streams and issuing the same actuator commands.

That display contains ten light values, four chemical values, eight contact values and seven proprioceptive values. Those are 29 raw coordinates. Actual energy and integrity arrive separately at the same five-times-per-second handoff used by P's bodily regulator. The controller can inspect past sensory history and its own previous commands. It cannot see global position, hidden stock, a source label, an analytic gradient or the mover's true velocity.

A human with history is intentionally more flexible than P's current encoding. The purpose is to ask whether useful information is available in the permitted window at all. We would first verify that the operator can use the display on simple disclosed control fixtures. Otherwise failure would tell us little about the sensors.

Imagine that this restricted controller approaches a source and establishes contact. That would be a witness of accessible information for that interaction. A paired trial from the same initial world would hide chemistry from the display while leaving the physical world unchanged. The actions might then differ, and so would the later world histories. We must not pretend those later streams remain matched. Separately, the original recorded history can be inspected with particular channels hidden, without running the world again.

Source state needs particular care. The source's appearance does not directly announce its stock, and even a depleted source emits chemicals. Chemical concentration also depends on where the body is and what happened earlier in the field. We can examine whether stock changes leave usable evidence; we cannot hand the controller an “empty” flag or demand information that the world never transmits.

The same approach would examine restorative contact, body-wall relation, mover motion and the difference between commanded and achieved movement. Failure would be reported as a failure of this controller in this setting unless a stronger physical or information argument establishes absence. No single unsuccessful controller proves that a sensory stream contains no information.

## Follow that information through P

Even if the raw sensory window contains a useful distinction, P may lose it. We would inspect a chain of records at matching times: raw receptors, their running means, residual input, cortical activity, the wave packet, packet centring, the query and returned associative content.

P takes native steps every 0.01 seconds. Every 0.2 seconds it summarizes cortical activity with a mean and an endpoint. Two different motions can have similar means and endpoints. During a mover passage or a turn by the body, we would retain the full native sequence alongside the compressed packet and identify such cases honestly. Some would be approximate similarities rather than exact mathematical equality. We would keep the distances and timing, not just two persuasive pictures.

Steady signals present another concern. Receptor centring operates over 30 seconds, and packet centring over 60 seconds. A sustained light, chemical or contact condition may become weaker along that route. We would inspect long enough holds and clearly labelled detached replay examples to see where the information remains. Direct contact/proprioceptive motor feedback and actual energy/integrity are separate routes; raw light existing in an observer's file is not a hidden tonic input to P.

This lets us say something precise: information was absent at the receptors; it was present but weak or ambiguous; cortical preprocessing lost it; or the packet lost it. The latter findings do not authorize adding a new input or changing the packet during commissioning. They may restrict what this configuration can test, or justify asking Jason about a different future branch.

## Give the fixed newborn process a bounded opportunity

After apparatus checks, the proposed small newborn assessment uses four predetermined birth stream IDs, with the unchanged birth law and the correct field history for each mover phase. Each observation lasts at most 600 simulated seconds, unless actual nonviability or an apparatus stop occurs first. No life has been launched.

We would record first source encounter, actual transfer, a net-positive energy interval, mechanical stress or damage, contact with restoration, useful repair while damaged, mover exposure, and the experience remaining after those events. These are different events. Touching repair while completely healthy cannot demonstrate integrity restoration.

Every assigned birth stays visible. A body that never encounters a source, dies before repair, or reaches the time limit remains in the record. We would not report repair opportunity only among the lucky animals that reached a wall. Secondary contact-time or damaged-time summaries can help explain opportunity, but cannot replace the unconditional denominator.

There is also an opposite risk: perhaps the innate process already leaves almost nothing to learn. We need to inspect that without mislabelling learning as innate behavior. Intact P begins changing immediately. Its later survival therefore cannot establish what an unchanged newborn process could sustain.

The draft proposes an additional diagnostic, subject to Jason's approval, that holds learned structure at its birth values while allowing the body's dynamics, sensory activity, filters, spontaneous process and other fast state to continue. It would be a deliberately modified test apparatus, always labelled separately from intact P. Its update timing needs review before implementation. If Jason declines that diagnostic, the honest result is that innate long-horizon maintenance remains unresolved.

Even with it, four finite observations cannot establish indefinite maintenance. Repeated successful circuits might reveal a concern, but making the world harder simply because one body lasts 600 seconds would be outcome-driven tuning. Likewise, making the world easier because one body dies would require an independently demonstrated apparatus defect, not disappointment.

## Observe whether the mechanisms have a chance to operate

P has eight units in each learned cortex, arranged in two groups of four. We would expose what the group shares and what individual rows differ on, the pressure forming those differences, the pressure reducing them, the opening state and the effect of support. Differences need not become useful to demonstrate that the specified operations are happening.

Low activity or similar weights alone would not establish that P needs more units. The relevant evidence would locate a necessary distinction in the raw input, show where the current representation loses it, and rule out absent encounters, adaptation, packet loss and arithmetic trouble. More width would remain a proposal for Jason, not an automatic rescue.

We would also keep contributions separate: sensory query, associative return, actual body/bias input, learned regulatory response, fresh exploration, motor evocation, direct body feedback, regulatory current and attenuation. A large norm is not necessarily influential if a receiver is saturated or contributions cancel. A small norm is not necessarily negligible. On a bounded set of recorded states, a detached one-step receiver calculation could show whether one contribution changes the receiver, without changing the living trajectory. There would be no balancing every route to the same magnitude.

Association is intentionally fading in this implementation. Its frozen-input contraction can be checked without expecting long autonomous associative chains. A changing driven signal need not decay monotonically. The absence of persistent chains would not refute Loom's broader ambition.

Energy and integrity credit would be displayed separately: actual bodily trend, the previous applied perturbation and features, eligibility, update and resulting contribution. Imagined or evoked integrity is not bodily repair. Whether the resulting response is helpful is a later scientific question. A harmful learned response is not a reason to rewrite credit during commissioning.

## Freeze the instrument before asking it to prove itself

The proposed records are substantial because the current concerns live in the native details. The final engineering measurement suggests about 11.67 machine-hours and 17.66 GB of compressed evidence for the full proposed horizon, before new diagnostic overhead and human control time. These are planning estimates from a short prior run, not measurements of commissioning. Jason can stage or reduce coverage; we should not quietly discard the temporal evidence to make the estimate smaller.

Three labels protect the interpretation. **Apparatus validity** concerns whether the body, world, sensors and records can pose the question. **Configuration observability** concerns whether a pathway is reached and capable of operating. **Scientific outcome** concerns whether what is learned is useful. Nuisance sensory distinctions, poor association, harmful credit or death despite available recovery can be scientific outcomes under a valid instrument. They must not become excuses for improving the settings.

After the checks, Jason would receive the evidence, failed attempts, remaining limitations and the scoped question that can honestly be tested. Only an explicit later ruling would freeze the complete coupling: laws, numerical values, anatomy, initialization, timing, measurement and interpretation. Learned state is supposed to change under those frozen rules.

Any later change that alters experience or interpretation would receive its own branch identity, preserving the original data. A genuine apparatus bug would invalidate its affected claims and require repair/revalidation; an inconvenient scientific result would stand. Accepting this design would not itself authorize execution or impose that freeze.

That is the proposed fairness test: establish real possibilities, trace the information actually supplied, retain every newborn's opportunity record, expose the mechanism's operations, and decide the boundary before useful outcomes can choose their own instrument.
