# Fixed passive interpretation plan

This is a nine-case, non-scientific motor/world exposure screen. It is not Founder Search, a developmental-efficacy experiment, a source-seeking policy test or a motor selection rule. All nine cases remain in the denominator, including terminal states, poor movement, excessive expenditure, damage and administrative censoring. No interim outcome changes a later case. A shared apparatus/resource/verification failure stops the batch, preserving later cases as unstarted. No retry, continuation or replacement.

After the full declared matrix has stopped, derive the following from existing Tier-1 native facts, events, compact wave motor diagnostics and checkpoints. No new world replay, deep neural replay or simulation is included.

- Per original start, display CURRENT, M1 and M2 side by side; retain all three starts without pooling away a contrary case.
- At ages 0, 10, 30, 60 and 90 seconds (or the actual stop), report maximum birth excursion, final displacement, native polyline path length, path/displacement ratio (undefined at zero displacement), and grid occupancy growth. Use 0.25 and 1 unit cells and the existing 0.25-cell half-offset sensitivity. Occupancy is a body-centre sample measure, not swept-volume coverage.
- Native endpoint speed, absolute angular speed, common/differential command RMS and left/right command correlation. Report signed forward velocity in the actual body frame. Forward/reverse bouts use +/-0.01 units/s and at least 0.1 s duration; retain the existing 0.005/0.02 sensitivity rather than choosing a successful threshold. Distinguish body-heading persistence from velocity-direction persistence; report direction correlations at 0.1, 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 8 and 16 s using speeds >0.01, with pair counts. Do not call a first correlation crossing a fitted diffusion constant.
- Re-entry after leaving a grid cell, repeated-space transitions, stationary fraction, and rotation/translation fractions: inactive if speed and body-radius*abs(omega) are both <=0.005; predominantly rotating if rotational edge speed >2*translation speed; predominantly translating for the opposite inequality. No spatial variable feeds a process.
- Exact recorded expenditure, separately effort and basal components under the unchanged expenditure law; initial/final E and I; all contact, impulses, damage, repair and transfers. Relate cost to movement descriptively, with no efficiency score selecting a winner.
- At recorded waves, inspect the actual spontaneous, direct-feedback, associative and current contributions, total pre-tanh input, attenuation used and achieved command. Report tanh susceptibility and attenuation as diagnostics; do not mistake component responsiveness for evidence that learning found useful control. Do not add perturbations to a world case.
- Source contact/transfer may appear in the descriptive record, but cannot determine candidate preference, parameter changes, stopping, selection or exclusions.

Discuss tradeoffs: increased displacement with excessive turning, contacts, cost or reduced susceptibility is not an unqualified improvement. Similar mean RMS does not make temporal exposure identical. Ninety seconds and three original starts cannot establish developmental benefit, robust environmental coverage or general superiority. A nonsignificant or mixed screen is not proof of equivalence. No winner is selected automatically; Jason reviews the retained evidence.

No p-value gate, useful-learning gate, survival gate, optimization objective or composite score is declared. The old FS evidence is a reference and is not relabelled as a new commissioning result.
