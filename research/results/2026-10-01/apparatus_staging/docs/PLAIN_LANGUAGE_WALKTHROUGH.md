# What the apparatus actually does

P itself is unchanged. The new code is the equipment around it: a clock with a hard stop, a recorder, two forms of external control, a deliberately modified diagnostic arm, and separate views for an operator and an evaluator.

For intact P, each real 0.01-second step still goes through the reviewed engine. At 0.2 seconds its normal handoff occurs. The apparatus records what happened and stops at the declared deadline. It does not turn a long observation into repeated 30-second permissions. A missing record or physical calculation failure is an apparatus failure, not a dead organism or a successful cutoff.

For physical-ceiling work, an external controller sees the permitted privileged body/world information. It follows a route written in advance and can adjust its two actuator commands using contact force. It cannot move the body directly, add reserves, refill a source or alter contact. Its actions are labelled `external_controller`. This build checked bounded commands, determinism, timing and input isolation; it did not show that the controller can complete an ecological circuit.

The sensor-only human sees a much smaller window: ten light, four chemical, eight contact and seven proprioceptive values. Actual energy and integrity arrive at five samples per second and remain held between samples. The operator can look back through raw history and their own commands. They receive no map, body pose, object name, source stock, mover phase or event notification. A decision holds the two commands for 0.1 bodily seconds. Looking at the screen adds no bodily time.

The fixed-structure arm has a separate, explicit name: **FIXED-STRUCTURE / NO-LASTING-PLASTICITY DIAGNOSTIC**. Its learned structure stays at its initial values while sensory activity, body dynamics, centring, traces, eligibility, exploration and other transient states continue. The recorder shows hypothetical updates that were discarded. The crucial timing is that a discarded bank update is removed before it can produce a control. Tests deliberately broke that timing and detected a later effect even after the weights had been restored.

The evaluator can follow a sensory value through raw input, receptor mean, residual, cortical activity, emitted packet, packet centring, trace, query and returned association. Step-start and step-end readings are marked separately. Shared and fine sensory structure, support and formation/coarsening pressures remain separate. Energy and integrity credit also remain separate.

At every handoff, detached receiver calculations remove one contribution at a time and compare the immediate result with the original. Native motor-only samples follow a fixed index rule. These calculations answer whether a contribution can change its receiver in that state. They do not produce another trajectory, infer benefit, or automatically balance gains.

There are three interpretation classes. AV concerns apparatus validity. CO describes opportunity and what the configuration makes observable. SO concerns scientific usefulness. Raw SO observations are retained, but the apparatus rejects them as grounds for adjusting configuration before freeze. Neither disappointing survival nor useful learning selects the settings.

The supplied review pages show only short manufactured fixtures. Use `Open Paused Sensor Reference.cmd` to inspect the restricted view. Use the separately named privileged review only as an evaluator. Both launch static saved pages and cannot advance a world. The live human gateway exists for a separately authorized future task; the delivered launchers do not attach it to a life.

Births 1, 2, 3 and 4 are still unstarted. Their eventual ceilings remain 600 seconds. No commissioning outcome, innate-performance claim or scientific result has been produced by this build.
