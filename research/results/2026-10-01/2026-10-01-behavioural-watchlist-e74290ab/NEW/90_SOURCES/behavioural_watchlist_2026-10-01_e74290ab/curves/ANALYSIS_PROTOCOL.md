## Declared descriptive conventions

- Windows are (start,end], at 60 and 600 seconds. Final partial windows report their actual exposure. Physical transfers/damage/repair are assigned by event endpoint; therefore their sums remain exact. Endpoint allocation can move at most one native interval across a window boundary. Path is cumulative native endpoint displacement, not integrated speed. Coverage uses 0.25-unit body-centre cells, with 1-unit sensitivity from the preserved audit. Re-entry counts cell transitions into previously visited cells, not time spent stationary.
- A behavioral source bout merges successive exact contact fragments for one source only if the gap is at most 1 second AND all intervening sampled body-to-source surface clearances are at most 0.25 units (half a body radius). Sensitivity crosses gaps 0.2, 1 and 5 seconds with clearance 0.05, 0.25 and 0.5 units. These are descriptive assay scales, not success criteria. All transfer and actual contact duration remain conserved under regrouping. A true geometric departure is clearance above 0.25 units before return; otherwise a separated contact is local recontact. A brief contact (<1 second actual contact) is labelled brush-like without claiming accident or intent. Sustained/productive means >=1 second and positive recorded transfer, not learned competence.
- Revisit is same-source recontact after true geometric departure; report immediate jitter separately. Contact time excludes noncontact gaps. Exposure is native duration within 1 surface unit of any source; sensitivity uses 0.5 and 2. No threshold is selected against a favorable result.
- Forward/reverse: body-frame speed above +0.01/below -0.01 world units/s. Bouts shorter than 0.1 s are retained in raw summaries but excluded from switch summaries. Velocity persistence pairs use actual timestamp matching; stationary endpoints excluded.
- Sensorimotor contingencies use first/last 900 seconds for complete lives, first/last thirds for censored/pilot lives, 1-second lag, 0.1-second native sample grid, and within-life exposure stratification. Pearson coefficients and sign fractions are descriptive, serially dependent, and have no p-values. Chemistry differences are receptor +45-degree minus receptor -45-degree, separately for each mixed chemical channel. Observer distances are outcomes/strata only. No model is trained.
- E-feature analysis uses exact stored nonlinear feature operands, fixed stored weights, exploration draws and need. An additive attribution uses a straight-line integrated derivative of tanh from the stored bias to the actual summed inputs, which has an exact scalar closed form. It is a bookkeeping decomposition, not unique causal attribution. Exact finite omissions are reported separately. No new draws or state updates occur.
- Event-aligned immediate omissions use recorded wave outputs. Learned E/I omissions are exact outgoing receiver algebra. Feature omission uses the nearest complete checkpoint, reports offset, and never interpolates weights. A 0.01-second motor relaxation *formula on stored operands* is allowed as a detached receiver calculation; no world, M1 process, clock or RNG advances. The last recorded motor input may precede newly outgoing controls: held inputs and old tendency are explicitly labelled, not passed off as the historical next command.
- Support exclusion: external E jumps are never intake. Natural windows further than 30 s from any intervention are primary confound checks, with 5 and 60 s sensitivity. Compare within E bands as well as age. Resurrection still selects which states are visited and enables otherwise impossible lifespan; exclusion cannot recover an unsupported counterfactual.
- Exploratory change points minimize two-segment squared error on complete 60-second window series, with at least five windows on each side. Every eligible metric/life is reported; no significance threshold, multiple-testing claim, or favorable-only selection.
- Future branch design is an unexecuted proposal, not an authority object. Any future branch requires separate approval and native-host-fault resolution.

## Availability limits

100 Hz native facts and event rows: physical geometry, raw 29 channels, actuator commands, transfers, damage/repair, sampled mover, stocks and reserves. Every 0.2-second completed wave: packets/psi/q, map norms/use/write/decay, learned logits/exploration/need/controls, cortical norms/activity/opening, regulator norms and motor diagnostics. Full checkpoints roughly every 60 seconds plus interventions: matrices, exact feature operands, association branch reads/projection and cortical formation/coarsening operands. Detailed continuous matrix trajectories and exact pre-impact velocity are not recorded at every event. Isolated impact normal closing speed can be inferred from its single normal impulse/mass; multi-contact pre-impact speed is left unavailable.

No inference of learning is licensed by a norm, correlation, contact, repeated support, or local omission alone. Analyses distinguish observed sequence, plausible mechanism and unresolved physical causal effect.
