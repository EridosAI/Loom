## Definitions fixed before data

- **Approach:** initial pose and first sampled entry into the broad south approach band 8 <= y < 9, with its mover pose, E/I and commands. For a start already in the band, report t=0. DETOUR reaches the same y-band in the left corridor; this is a longitudinal marker, not mover contact.
- **Crossing corridor:** CROSS nominal x=10; WAIT nominal x=6. Report lateral displacement and maximum absolute lateral deviation throughout. Predeclared corridor tolerance is 0.5 m for interpretation only; do not feed it to the controller. Out-of-corridor passage is a deviated physical route, not silently the prescribed corridor witness.
- **Entry and exit:** for northward CROSS/WAIT, entry is the first native sample with centre y >= 9 (body enters the mover's vertical collision band); full exit is the first subsequent native sample with centre y > 11. Report the bracketing native times/positions and later re-entries. Do not invent an exact sub-step crossing time by interpolation. Equality is a geometric marker, not proof of contact. A complete prescribed crossing requires full exit within the lateral corridor. Also report centreline crossing y=10 separately.
- **Detour:** retain exact nominal endpoints (1.5,6) and (1.5,14), the A0 swept-union clearance certificate, actual x deviations, y=9 and y>11 side-passage times and destination arrival. The always-clear body-centre corridor |x-1.5| <= 0.25, 6 <= y <= 14 remains statically clear; nominal mover-union clearance is 3 m, corridor lower margin 2.75 m. Actual deviations outside that corridor must be disclosed; collision-free deviated movement is not automatically the declared corridor witness.
- **Arrival:** first native sample within Euclidean 0.25 m of the declared destination, irrespective of speed; report speed/orientation, remaining time, later departure and final distance. This reporting radius is not a controller gain, body size, stop condition or new physics. Absence by the ceiling is a controller/horizon miss unless stronger evidence exists.
- **Waiting:** WAIT prescribed clock interval [0,12); report actual last waiting/first proceeding decision times, all commands during it, time with both |commands| <= 1e-12, displacement from initial centre, E/I and cost, and first sampled northward displacement >0.01 m. CROSS and DETOUR have zero prescribed waiting; report any observed stationary time separately (native endpoint speed <=1e-6, clearly labelled sampled). No post-hoc wait extension.
- **Clearance:** compute signed disk-to-rectangle gap for every saved native body position and mover pose evaluated at the same saved time from the unchanged bound law. Report minimum, timestamp, body pose and mover pose for whole case and crossing/wait portions. Separately report the minimum at recorded controller decision poses, where the mover rectangle/velocity was actually sampled by the apparatus. Native-law evaluation is derived geometry, not an extra simulation or an actual recorded 100 Hz mover channel. Finite sample minima are not certified continuous-time minima. Event contacts/impulses establish actual contact; do not infer contact from a small sampled gap or infer no contact merely from positive sample gaps.
- **Mover:** retain phase parameter, wrapped phase-at-time, analytic path law, every actually sampled rectangle/velocity in the controller stream, and event collider identifiers. Report these at approach, entry/exit, release and arrival with sample times. A viewer may draw the analytic path and the recorded sampled poses with distinct labels.
- **Costs and travel:** sum all positive-duration event expenditures and all source transfers; retain starting/final E/I, all damage/repair and terminal causes. Report waiting, moving and total cost separately. Travel time is t(arrival)-t(0); WAIT also reports t(arrival)-12 if applicable. Actual recorded polyline length is the sum over native positions from the initial point, separately through arrival and through final record; label it sampled path length, not exact continuous arclength. Do this for all cases, highlighting DETOUR.
- **Contact:** retain every collider, contact interval, actual impact impulse, sustained force/impulse, impact damage and sustained stress damage. Distinguish the mover from every static collider. Preserve small contacts, recontacts, timing misses and excessive waiting. Do not conflate summed sustained impulses with a single collision impulse. Retain exact accounting operands rather than estimating damage from sampled proximity.

Use absolute time tolerance 1e-10 (existing event-time tolerance) symmetrically for nominal clock-boundary classifications. Keep original floating timestamps. An accounting event spanning a reporting boundary is marked straddling; retain its exact operands and bounds, report duration-proportional cost allocation only where the event's constant commanded cost rate supports it, and label that derived allocation. Never rewrite event times or claim an event-boundary E/I value that was not recorded. Check all complete 0.2 s accounting windows and separately any partial tail; positive net E or useful learning is not a gate.
