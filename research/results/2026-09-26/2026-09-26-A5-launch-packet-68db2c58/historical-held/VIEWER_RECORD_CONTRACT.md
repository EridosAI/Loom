No live renderer, server, UI feedback path or simulation replay is proposed or started. This is the record contract for a future resolved and authorized case. There is no A5 time-series plot yet because there is no A5 trajectory.

Wave rows are empty for the inactive-neural external arm; the manifest's legacy scientific selection stride is not permission to thin native physics. Full fields live in initial/checkpoint/final states; native field hashes preserve their identities. Do not add object-label inputs to P or invent ray-hit labels.

Plot display aggregation, if later needed, must never replace full stored arrays, hide brief contacts or alter analysis. Saved-data validation must check payload hashes, initial snapshot identity, native/event continuity, issued-versus-delivered commands, ledgers, milestone bounds and inactive neural state using records. No dynamic replay, controller recomputation, sensor recomputation or new prehistory is allowed by preparation or the proposed read-only reporting allowance.
