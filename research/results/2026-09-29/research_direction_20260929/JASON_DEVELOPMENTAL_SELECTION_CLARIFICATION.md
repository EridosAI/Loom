==================================================
DEVELOPMENTAL SELECTION CLARIFICATION
==================================================

The prohibition on inherited/prior learned state is intended to prohibit
EXTERNALLY AUTHORED COMPETENCE:

- offline/artificial training supplied by us;
- hand-designed learned weights;
- an externally prepared source-finding policy;
- privileged experience the organism/lineage never actually lived;
- semantic task knowledge inserted into the organism.

It is NOT intended to prohibit:

- selecting which genuinely blank-start organism receives further
  attention;
- continuing the same organism after it develops useful competence;
- checkpointing that organism's legitimately acquired state;
- branching a checkpoint into multiple future continuations;
- later lineage-derived starts whose internal structure was acquired only
  through lawful organism-world experience.

Selection is external allocation of research attention.
It is NOT a reward/input/plasticity signal to the organism.

Chance encounters are legitimate parts of developmental history.

A bug that succeeds because an early random action happened to produce a
useful encounter is not contaminated by that luck. The important question
is whether its subsequent internal development makes later useful
interaction more likely.

==================================================
TERMINOLOGY
==================================================

Use these distinctions in future documents:

BLANK START
A new organism with the baseline unlearned initialization.

CONTINUING INDIVIDUAL
The same organism/state continuing its own developmental history.

LINEAGE BRANCH
An exact checkpoint of a legitimately developed organism forked into two
or more future continuations. Each branch shares the same lawful history
up to the fork.

LINEAGE-DERIVED START
If later used: a new physical continuation initialized with some
lineage-acquired persistent structure. This must be identified explicitly
and must not be called a blank-start cohort.

EXTERNAL PRETRAINING
Structure supplied from experience/training that did not arise through
that lineage's lawful organism-world interaction. This remains outside the
intended baseline.

Do not silently decide which internal states a lineage-derived start would
retain/reset. That is a later design question requiring Jason's review.

==================================================
FOUNDER CONCEPT
==================================================

A future Founder-0 means:

a blank-start organism whose own lawful lived experience has produced
persistent useful competence worth continuing.

Founder-0 does NOT need to solve the full ecology.

Early candidate evidence might include history-dependent improvement in
one or more things such as:
- productive source interaction;
- approach/contact control;
- depletion/departure behaviour;
- repair interaction;
- mover handling;
- continued viability.

Do not turn this illustrative list into a founder scoring rule yet.

Do not average away rare developmental success simply because most blank
organisms fail.
