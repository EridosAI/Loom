## 3. Proposed population scale and reasoning

Recommend **12 distinct blank births**, one at a time, under the existing master seed and anatomy. Twelve provides three times the independent-life opportunities of the old four-birth roster, with 7,200 organism-seconds in the first common window. It is a practical search allocation, not a power calculation or a claim that rare development will be found.

| Design option, not an additional arm | Common observation | At historical A2/A3 wall rates, as an external-controller comparison only | Main tradeoff |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 8 births × 600 s | 4,800 s | 15.67–15.91 h | Cheaper; narrower opportunity coverage |
| **12 births × 600 s** | **7,200 s** | **23.51–23.87 h** | Recommended compromise; intact-P planning allowance is higher |
| 16 births × 600 s | 9,600 s | 31.35–31.83 h | More births, with proportionally greater recording and review cost |

For intuition only, if an independent life had an unknown 5% chance of an interesting trajectory within this window, 12 lives would have about a 46% chance of at least one; at 1%, only about 11%. These are hypothetical probabilities, not estimates from Loom. Shared anatomy and one ecology further limit generalization. Twelve negative lives would not establish absence of rare development.

No anatomy search, outcome-selected seed, replacement birth for a death, source-directed placement or retrospective exclusion is proposed. Fewer than 12 completed windows because of an apparatus/resource stop must be reported as such.

**Why this timing?** Jason chose a 600-second first window and later total ages of 900 and 1,800 seconds to give development more time. The first window covers twenty mover periods, twenty receptor-mean time constants, ten packet-mean time constants and two 300-second opening time constants. In the ordinary opening law this corresponds to approximately 86% opening from zero, not maturity or a success gate. The later checkpoints allow a further 300 then 900 seconds of authentic experience. The source renewal constant is 400 seconds, no-intake reserve exhaustion depends on activity, and slow sensory/bank references have 5,000–10,000-second constants. Even 1,800 seconds does not assess their asymptotic behavior. Longer observation supplies opportunity, not guaranteed development; a terminal organism cannot be kept alive to reach its allowance. [R6–R8, R13]

No organism is stopped early for achieving a positive witness. The common initial window is offered to each declared birth unless terminal or predeclared apparatus/resource conditions intervene. Later continuation starts at the **exact saved endpoint**. Simulation, neural, field and RNG clocks remain continuous; a week of researcher deliberation would add zero simulated time.

## 6. Observation set: experience, change and later use

Preserve a complete record for every declared birth, including no encounter, accidental benefit, damage, death, stagnation, unusual motion and administrative censoring. Publish the full roster and actual observed durations before selected-case illustrations. Use unconditional birth counts alongside time-at-risk and exposure denominators; never show survivors alone as the population.

Each dossier separates three evidence layers:

| Layer | Observation | Insufficient inference |
|---|---|---|
| **A — useful or harmful event** | Actual contact, resolved intake/repair, expense, damage and reserve consequences, with preceding movement | An accidental useful event is not learned competence |
| **B — internal change** | Time-ordered changes in sensory structure/references, H/use, banks, eligibility and transient state | A changed parameter or rising norm is not useful development |
| **C — later consequence** | Later expression of changed pathways, accompanying a useful regulatory/behavioral consequence; event context, alternatives and uncertainty retained | Correlation or immediate receiver influence alone is not complete causal proof |

For each plausible A→B→C account, retain exact time/index references, before/after intervals, repeated encounters if any, actual E/I consequences and competing explanations such as changed stock, pose, contact geometry, injury, direct feedback, exploration or current reserve need. Select illustrations only after retaining the complete roster; label post-hoc windows as exploratory. Do not invent a universal numerical threshold for persistence, competence or similarity. An immediate adjustment within the same contact can be meaningful regulation but cannot by itself establish a lasting learned contribution.

Observer-only ecological timelines include first and subsequent source contacts, gross debit/credit, expenditure, net E over explicitly delimited intervals, source depletion/renewal, departure/recontact, damage and impulses, repair contact, eligible repair while I<1 and actual restoration. Mover/wall events, movement, command/achieved-motion mismatch, travel, near encounters and remaining reserves contextualize opportunity. Source/repair identities and geometric annotations stay in analysis, never in P.

For encounter sparsity, retain continuous source/body separation and lawful raw contrasts around approaches, not just successful transfer. Geometric nearness is not proof that an actionable cue was available; contact is not proof that it was productive. Report actual sensory/packet paths around missed as well as successful encounters, with pre-encounter reserves and post-encounter opportunity. No new B4 assay or independent ecological trajectory is added.

**P-specific records and interpretation:**

| Domain | Retained observations | Necessary distinction |
|---|---|---|
| Sensory cortex / D1 | Native raw, receptor means/residuals, activity, shared/fine structure, references, saturation and spectra with numerical uncertainty | Low rank or large change alone is not loss or learning |
| Pooling/support / D2 | Opening, support, formation, coarsening, reference forces, projections and actual increments | Pool membership is fixed by configuration; changing support/weights is not anatomical reassignment |
| Native history / D3–D4 | Step-start versus endpoint alignment; wave mean-plus-endpoint packets, means, beta, traces and query | Observer access to a tonic raw signal does not imply a tonic learner channel; no bypass added |
| Association H/use / D5–D6 | Writes/decay, map use, gates, query, sweeps, q, contribution terms and exact immediate receiver dependencies | Fading activity and lasting map change differ; used H is not necessarily useful |
| Regulator / D5,D7 | Separate E/I credit, old perturbations/features, eligibility, bank/reference changes, learned contribution versus fresh exploration and bodily/bias terms | Actual reserve credit is not an externally defined reward or evaluator judgment |
| Motor expression / D5 | Oscillator/noise, direct feedback, evocation, current, attenuation, resulting command and achieved motion | A neural contribution can be numerically real yet behaviorally unhelpful |

Retain current native fidelity and every-handoff wave evidence. The current D5 implementation evaluates wave receivers every handoff and motor receivers at handoffs plus the declared native-index stride of 100 (in this cadence the latter indices also fall on handoffs). Do not revert to the older draft's at-most-100-wave-sample proposal. Detached receiver omissions establish local influence in a frozen receiver, not alternate behavior or benefit; they must not evolve a world or consume live RNG. [R4, R5, R8]

A sensory-specific claim requires evidence linking lasting shared/fine/reference change to a later sensory distinction and its effective downstream use, followed by a plausible useful consequence. A learned bank acting mainly through current bodily state could support history-dependent regulation while leaving lasting sensory contribution unresolved. Keep both conclusions in separate dossier fields.

## 7. Founder-candidate semantics

| Term | Meaning and allowed claim |
|---|---|
| BLANK START | The ordinary unlearned initialization described above; no externally authored competence |
| CONTINUING INDIVIDUAL | The same organism/world continuing its actual history and complete accumulated state |
| FOUNDER CANDIDATE | A blank-start individual whose own experience appears to have produced persistent useful developmental change worth further observation |
| FOUNDER-0 | A later explicitly selected continuing individual, if the evidence warrants Jason's decision |
| LINEAGE BRANCH | A later exact fork sharing an authentic historical prefix; not created or designed in detail here |

Assign a provisional candidate description only when a dossier provides a plausible B→C connection grounded in the actual history. A alone is an encounter witness; B alone is plasticity without demonstrated utility. Confidence can remain limited. No clean causal proof is demanded from every individual, and no aggregate score, fixed success percentile, source-hit count or survival rank defines candidacy.

Selection for observation is broader than candidacy: an ambiguous or no-encounter individual may deserve continuation without being called a founder candidate. A terminal organism can preserve useful developmental evidence but cannot become a continuing founder. Most lives can fail while one provides interesting development; the failures remain part of the result. Neither a good trajectory nor the word "candidate" automatically creates Founder-0 or authorizes branching.

## 8. Selection-for-continuation procedure

At each review boundary, first seal all trajectories and complete a compact A/B/C-plus-opportunity dossier for **every** birth, with no scalar ranking. Record the choice and rationale before any continuation executes. Jason reviews the dossiers and approves exact selected endpoints separately.

For the proposed first four slots:

1. Allocate up to three to distinct developmental questions suggested by the records: plausible useful B→C, internal change with uncertain later use, persistent regulation with unresolved sensory contribution, or an unusual trajectory exposing a specific limitation. These are evidence families, not required behavioral tasks. Early source contact alone is insufficient.
2. Reserve one slot for an **opportunity comparator** among the other viable, coherently checkpointed individuals, irrespective of productive outcome. Select it by a deterministic hash ordering of the declared birth IDs and a fixed published selection label, established before any outcomes. If fewer than four are eligible, preserve the smaller count; do not create replacements. All remaining eligible individuals participate in this neutral choice, including no-encounter cases.
3. For indistinguishable rationale-based choices, use that same predeclared neutral order. Publish exclusions, reasons, state identities and actual coverage. No slot must be filled merely to hit a count; unused slots are not transferred to extra births or more time.

The opportunity comparator is a proposed protection against attention collapsing onto early contact; a single such slot cannot make the continued subset representative. Analysis must separate rationale-selected and neutral continuations. Eligibility requires a nonterminal state and a valid checkpoint, not high E/I, low damage or success.

At the second review, propose at most one development-directed continuation and one neutral eligible continuation from the remaining first-stage continuations. Again review all twelve original dossiers, including censored/dead lives, when interpreting the selected subset. If fewer eligible individuals remain, use fewer slots. If there is no defensible scientific reason to spend the next stage, pause the programme for Jason rather than automatically use the budget.

The neutral order's exact label, the selected endpoints and resource grants belong to a later reviewed launch/continuation packet. None is generated here. These allocations are caps on research attention, not criteria that a fixed fraction of organisms must satisfy.

No selection action edits the engine, restocks sources, resets fields/noise/means, increases reserves or tells the organism that it was selected. No hindsight replay replaces the actual history. A selected record is labeled administratively selected/censored; selection must not be confused with an additional biological mechanism.

## 10. Interpretation matrix

| Observed pattern | Bounded interpretation | What does not follow |
|---|---|---|
| Useful event, little supported later change | Lawful chance opportunity, possibly immediate regulation | Learning or founder candidacy |
| Some individuals show persistent useful B→C | Evidence of developmental change in those histories; assess regulation and sensory contribution separately | General competence or a population success rate at longer ages |
| One rare strong trajectory | A provisional founder candidate may merit continuation | The other lives were expendable, or Founder-0 is automatically selected |
| Useful experiences recur but change is absent | Available experiences were not visibly retained in examined pathways/time | No possible learning at any horizon |
| Internal change occurs but behavior is unchanged or worse | Plasticity without demonstrated utility, possibly harmful change | The mechanism should be tuned until useful |
| Many deaths, a minority develops | Development can coexist with a high failure burden; report all denominators | Population-level success, robust survival or a reason to erase failures |
| No sufficient useful experience before stop | Opportunity remains unresolved; inspect exposures and censoring | A nursery is required or P cannot exploit experience |
| No contact but actual cue/approach opportunities existed | Examine motor exploration, signal transformation and available reserves; scarcity is not yet the sole explanation | Empty encounter count proves sparse resources |
| Substantial repeated lawful experience, usable signals and time, but no effective lasting use | Bounded concern that present P cannot exploit the supplied experiences | A definitive mechanism refutation, permission to replace P, or an automatic nursery |
| Useful regulation with no supported sensory structural contribution | First scientific question may have evidence; second remains unresolved | Lasting sensory learning was shown |
| Apparatus or recording failure | Scientific coverage is incomplete; retain last coherent state and fault | Biological failure, negative developmental evidence or permission to retry |
| Horizon/resource pause before persistence can be observed | Administratively censored individual | Absence of later persistence |

No zero-founder result selects the next research branch automatically. Observational A/B/C accounts remain exploratory and vulnerable to changing physical context. A later causal intervention may be proposed if needed; none is part of this population's execution scope.
