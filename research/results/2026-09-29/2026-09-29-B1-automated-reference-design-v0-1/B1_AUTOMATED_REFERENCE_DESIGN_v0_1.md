# B1 automated reference replacement — v0.1, for Jason's review

**DESIGN ONLY. No implementation, training, new fixture/prehistory generation, rollout or execution authority is approved or created.** This replaces the proposed human instrument for B1, not P, the Base World, sensor laws or the developmental-selection direction. All numerical choices below are proposed assay settings for review, not commissioned settings.

Jason withdrew B1-CHEMISTRY-HIDDEN before Start. It is preserved as **AUTHORIZED-BUT-NOT-EXECUTED / WITHDRAWN AT JASON'S DIRECTION**, with zero simulated seconds, native steps and commands. Its earlier authorization, prepared world and started wall timer remain in the audit record; “not executed” does not mean the service was never prepared. The service is stopped. FULL-RAW remains the unchanged bounded human observation: 20.2 simulated seconds and 202 human commands. Its files were verified against the existing preservation inventory; no new outcome assessment was made. Neither human record nor the old sealed B1 starting state is a training or test input to this replacement. Evaluator custody remains closed.

Jason's stated reason for retirement is that manual performance is dominated by learning this numerical interface and its short command holds, making the proposed human pair a poor instrument for its intended question. This is an instrument decision, not a finding that sensory information is absent or that P failed.

**The three questions and the smallest useful design.** Use one modest causal model family in two input conditions, with a passive decoding readout and an active actuator readout. The passive assay tests source-relevant information; the active assay tests whether a frozen external controller can use permitted history to produce source interaction. Compare FULL and CHEMISTRY-HIDDEN on matched starts. Do not add repair, mover competence, survival, cortical/packet accessibility, learning efficacy or Founder Search objectives.

| Question | Proposed evidence | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Is source-relevant information recoverable? | Predict body-relative direction and distance to the nearest energy-source surface from held-out permitted histories; compare with training-prior and no-sensory controls. | This is recoverability by this probe on these starts, not unique state reconstruction, stock recognition or an information-theoretic ceiling. |
| Is it usable in closed loop? | Frozen raw-history controller produces supported source contact and resolved source-debit/body-credit transfer. | A positive witness concerns the external reference, not P. A miss may be a controller limitation. |
| What changes without chemistry? | Paired decoding errors on identical physical prefixes, and paired source-interaction outcomes after independently controlled continuations. | Bounded input-condition dependence, not universal necessity. Later physical histories will differ. |

**Runtime information boundary.** The reference gets the existing 10 light, 4 chemistry, 8 contact and 7 proprioceptive coordinates at their actual 0.01-second cadence; actual E/I only at the existing 0.2-second handoff, held between handoffs; causal timestamps and its own past commands. No human annotations or externally supplied notes enter the model. CHEMISTRY-HIDDEN omits all four chemistry coordinates from current and past inputs, preprocessing and every controller-facing payload, leaving 25 real coordinates. E/I and all other channels are unchanged. The full physical chemistry and private raw record remain intact.

At each decision the model consumes only rows already available, then emits one bounded left/right pair in [-1,1] for the existing 0.1-second hold. Inference wall time does not advance the world. There is no future sampling, command batching, adaptive hold length, world map, pose, source ID/stock, material label, analytic gradient, privileged contact flag, seed or fixture identifier in its input. Normalization uses training data only. A zero pair still advances a hold. Neither a probe target nor a teacher action is supplied to the reference at test time.

The trusted runner owns the world and recordings. A separate controller worker receives only serialized allowed history and a frozen model; it has no Engine object, evaluator callback, private-record path or snapshot access. Isolation and input tests must be demonstrated before a new apparatus checkpoint is accepted. Predictions formed internally from allowed history are permitted; a truth-derived runtime helper is not. All P neural activity/plasticity remains inactive in this external-controller assay. Apparatus weights must never be inserted into P or described as a Founder or lineage-derived P state.

**Reference and training, bounded in advance.** Proposed architecture: one 32-state GRU with a linear source-vector readout and a two-output tanh actuator readout. Train separate FULL and HIDDEN instances with the same architecture, fixed seed and training allowance; hidden chemistry is excluded before fitting, including normalization. Each state begins at zero; no teacher or privileged history primes it. Trained weights can encode prior experience from the training split, as Jason explicitly allows for commissioning apparatus.

Use offline supervised fitting from fixed privileged-teacher trajectories, not an open-ended reinforcement-learning campaign. The teacher is an external data-construction instrument using the existing paired actuators, mechanics and unchanged waypoint/contact gains. Its exact route construction and source-target rule are fixed before collecting data. Its failed trajectories remain in the dataset. No end-to-end P training occurs.

The source label is the two-component body-relative vector to the closest point on the nearest energy-source surface, regardless of stock; evaluator-only tie handling is deterministic. This deliberately does not equate a source with available stock. Distance and angular error are reported separately, with bearing undefined at zero displacement. The action target is the teacher's command for that decision. Source and action losses are separately normalized on training data and equally weighted. Proposed fitting bound: one fit per input condition, Adam at 0.001, at most 100 completed epochs or 60 wall minutes per model; choose the minimum combined development-loss checkpoint among completed epochs. No architecture, seed or learning-rate search. Fix remaining implementation details, label conventions and deterministic settings in the reviewed build specification before training; there is no training approval in this document.

Two decoding controls use the same source target: a training-set constant prediction, and an otherwise matched time/own-command-only model. The latter gets neither raw channels nor E/I and has both readouts; it also supplies the sensory-free action control below. Fit it once under the same bound. Report its decoding performance alongside FULL and HIDDEN. If it performs similarly, do not attribute prediction to sensory information. Probe capacity and priors can otherwise make a decoding score misleading; this control rationale is informed by [Hewitt and Liang, 2019](https://aclanthology.org/D19-1275/), whose experiments concern language representations, not Loom.

**A fixed sensing prefix avoids teacher-action leakage into the primary decoding assay.** Every constructed trajectory begins with the same proposed 2-second sequence: five holds each of (0.3,0.3), (0.2,0.4), (0.4,0.2), then (0,0). These are commands, not guaranteed velocities. No source truth chooses or adjusts this prefix. Its motion, contacts, costs and any early stop are retained. Train the source readout at the 0.1-second decision boundaries within this prefix; the primary held-out queries are fixed at 0 and 2 seconds, before any teacher-selected or model-selected action enters that history. This prevents a privileged teacher's past target-directed commands from being mistaken for information in the sensory interface. Report pre-contact versus already-contacting endpoints separately using offline evaluator labels; do not change controller inputs or query times based on those labels. If a case ends before a query, mark that query unobserved and retain the case; do not fill it from another run.

After the common prefix, the frozen actuator head controls the remaining 28 seconds. Teacher action supervision uses subsequent decision points in the training split. Train/development teacher trajectories also use the same prefix. Source decoding during later closed-loop movement may be reported descriptively, but cannot replace the predeclared prefix test. Imitation can fail when its own actions lead away from the training distribution; that motivates a controller-limitation interpretation, not an automatic data-aggregation or retraining phase. See [Ross, Gordon and Bagnell, 2011](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v15/ross11a.html).

**Held-out construction and one batch.** Proposed cap: 16 training starts, 4 development starts and 12 held-out test starts. All are explicitly manufactured commissioning starts in the unchanged Base World, not blank-start P organisms. Preserve lawful field/prehistory construction and native laws; do not relocate or increase resources, alter mover parameters, reset reserves during a trajectory or create a nursery.

Split whole start/prehistory families before any trajectory collection. Nearby variants, checkpoint continuations and overlapping histories stay in one split. No frame-level train/test split, shared hidden warm-up, fitting of normalizers on held-out rows or reuse of the disclosed human fixture. Predeclare four held-out starts in each of three source-distance bands within a static finite-body approach envelope, with body orientation and mover/field phase varied. Freeze exact bands, admissibility tests and deterministic draw order before constructing starts. Conditions must be selected on declared geometry/lawful-state criteria, never on probe scores or successful trial outcomes. Static admissibility is not proof of dynamic feasibility.

For each held-out start, allocate exactly three independent 30-second-ceiling trajectories from the same complete snapshot:

1. FULL reference.
2. CHEMISTRY-HIDDEN reference.
3. The frozen time/own-command-only action control, with all raw channels and E/I absent. Its learned command sequence is a baseline, not a sensory-access witness.

The third trajectory uses the same prefix and a fresh recurrent state. Without sensory feedback its subsequent sequence is independent of the actual scene, except that the external runner enforces the same legal stops. This distinguishes a useful sensory-reference result from an equally effective learned open-loop sequence. Do not give the null model privileged stopping reasons or bodily status as inputs. No reference miss earns a replacement start, extra episode or new teacher route.

Thus there are **24 primary automated sensory cases**, 12 sensory-free action controls and 20 training/development data trajectories: at most **56 × 30 = 1,680 simulated seconds**. Each model/test starts with fresh recurrent state; there is no human carryover or online weight update. Pair physical/RNG state and the common command prefix, then let lawful trajectories diverge. Validate prefix equality; do not force later states or random streams to agree by intervention. Twelve pairs are a bounded coverage sample, not a population guarantee or statistical power claim.

Prior A-series commissioning supplies bounded physical witnesses, not a guarantee that every new start admits interaction within 30 seconds. The training/development teacher records provide additional limited calibration. To keep this design small, there is no fourth, privileged held-out control arm. A held-out miss therefore cannot by itself separate inadequate physical opportunity from inadequate control. Preserve static admissibility and contact/cost records for later review; any further physical witness requires separate scope and authorization.

**Report and interpretation.** Use one row per held-out start, including all failures and cutoffs. Report prefix vector/distance/bearing errors, control baselines, whether contact already occurred, each arm's source contact and supported duration, resolved gross transfer, time to interaction, expenditure, net E, starting/final E/I, damage, commands and exact stop category. Keep a contact-only event separate from a positive-transfer interaction. Positive net E and survival are not pass gates. Preserve every native record and uncertainty at transfer/sign boundaries.

Summarize counts and paired differences, while retaining the 12 case rows. Correlated native frames are not independent cases. A single interaction is an existence witness; wider coverage must be stated from the actual cases. Multiple independent-start witnesses are more informative, but no universal success percentage or scientific efficacy gate is introduced. No finite model failure establishes information-theoretic absence. The reviewed report, rather than an automatic score, decides whether catastrophic perceptual-access uncertainty is sufficiently narrowed for subsequent commissioning.
