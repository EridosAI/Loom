# Jason's Nursery-0 design addendum — exploration dynamics

Verbatim user instruction received during this design task:

**NURSERY-0 DESIGN ADDENDUM — EXPLORATION DYNAMICS**

Continue the Nursery-0 design work already underway. **Do not restart or discard completed work.**

However, before finalising the recommended Nursery-0 configuration, add one additional analysis/design question:

**Is the apparent developmental-opportunity bottleneck partly caused by poor spatial exploration from the newborn spontaneous motor process, rather than arena/source sparsity alone?**

The existing Founder Search bugs typically accumulated approximately 13–14 world units of path length while often remaining relatively close to their birth region. This may indicate substantial motor activity but poor exploratory displacement.

Before locking the nursery geometry, perform a passive audit of the preserved current-world trajectories. No new simulation is authorised.

Quantify where possible:

- maximum excursion from birth;
- spatial coverage and coverage growth with age;
- mean-square displacement versus elapsed time;
- translational speed;
- angular speed;
- heading/direction autocorrelation or persistence time;
- path curvature;
- frequency of reversals;
- left/right actuator correlation;
- duration of sustained forward-motion bouts;
- fraction of activity spent predominantly rotating versus translating;
- recurrence into already visited space;
- evidence of wall/geometry trapping.

Use this to distinguish, as far as the records permit:

1. insufficient movement magnitude;
2. excessive turning/reversal;
3. insufficient directional persistence;
4. environmental trapping;
5. another identifiable cause.

Do not assume limited final displacement means insufficient movement.

If the organisms are travelling substantial distance but exploring poorly, add **newborn spontaneous-motor temporal structure** as an explicit Nursery-0 design dial.

In that case, prefer considering changes such as:

- longer correlated locomotor bouts;
- slowly varying left/right imbalance;
- broad wandering trajectories rather than tight local loops;
- occasional spontaneous reorientation;
- natural low-activity/rest periods;

rather than merely increasing actuator magnitude.

The purpose would be to make spontaneous activity produce meaningful exposure to the world, **not to supply competence**.

Do NOT introduce:

- source seeking;
- novelty reward;
- distance-from-birth reward;
- unvisited-space detection;
- target coordinates;
- semantic `EXPLORE` actions;
- privileged environmental information.

Structured spontaneous activity remains endogenous and blind to what is useful.

This new consideration should be analysed jointly with the existing Nursery-0 dials:

- arena/source opportunity;
- energy developmental runway;
- integrity resilience;
- repair opportunity.

In particular, note that improving exploration may allow a **larger nursery** than would otherwise be required, potentially avoiding the increased wall/impact frequency caused by simply shrinking the arena.

Preserve all already-developed candidate nursery designs as provisional. Revise them only if this exploration audit materially changes the interpretation.

If the evidence is insufficient to justify changing spontaneous activity, say so and leave it unchanged.

**Do not modify P, the motor generator, world geometry, or any numerical parameter in this task.**

Incorporate the audit and its implications into the final Nursery-0 design package before making the recommendation.

DESIGN ONLY. No simulation, authority, push, PR or merge.
