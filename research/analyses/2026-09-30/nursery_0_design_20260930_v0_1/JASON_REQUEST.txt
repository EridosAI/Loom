==================================================
IMPORTANT NEW DESIGN CONCERN
==================================================

Making the arena smaller / increasing encounter density will also increase
the frequency of:

- wall contact;
- mover encounters;
- damaging impacts;
- other physical interaction.

That may be useful for learning, but it can also shorten developmental
runway before the organism gets enough energy experience.

Therefore Nursery-0 must jointly consider:

ECOLOGICAL OPPORTUNITY
and
ORGANISM RESILIENCE / DEVELOPMENTAL RUNWAY.

The current numerical settings were a starting configuration, not assumed
optimal biology.

Do not preserve an arbitrary numerical value merely because it happened
to be the first implementation.

==================================================
CORE NURSERY PRINCIPLE
==================================================

The nursery should make it plausible for one blank organism to experience:

source opportunity
-> productive contact
-> bodily energy consequence
-> departure
-> another source opportunity / revisit
-> later related experience

AND ALSO:

ordinary impact/contact
-> integrity consequence
-> continued survival
-> later structural encounter
-> possible avoidance / softening / repair interaction

within ONE continuing life.

The organism needs enough time and consequence density for experience to
compound.

==================================================
DO NOT MAKE THE NURSERY TRIVIAL
==================================================

The goal is NOT:

- indefinite survival by random movement;
- harmless collisions;
- effectively unlimited energy;
- source everywhere;
- removal of integrity consequence;
- removal of need;
- a curriculum that puts food directly in front of the bug;
- source labels / arrows / correct actions / semantic reward.

The bug must still be able to:

- miss things;
- collide;
- lose energy;
- suffer integrity damage;
- make poor choices;
- die.

But premature death should not dominate so strongly that development has
almost no opportunity to occur.

==================================================
DESIGN TWO COUPLED SETS OF DIALS
==================================================

A. ECOLOGICAL-OPPORTUNITY DIALS

Consider:

- arena linear scale / accessible area;
- number and density of energy sources;
- source spacing / placement law;
- restorative-surface availability;
- mover placement relative to recurrent useful regions;
- free space required for avoidance, detour and retreat.

Keep the existing source/material/chemical laws unless a later explicit
decision changes them.

B. RESILIENCE / DEVELOPMENTAL-RUNWAY DIALS

Explicitly examine which current numerical values are merely provisional
and could reasonably be adjusted to let organisms survive long enough to
develop.

At minimum consider separately:

1. birth energy reserve;
2. basal expenditure magnitude;
3. actuation-effort cost;
4. integrity damage scaling / stress tolerance;
5. graded capability loss as integrity falls;
6. repair effectiveness / usable damaged-state recovery.

Do NOT collapse these into one "easy mode" parameter.

Do NOT change all of them together by default.

For every proposed resilience change explain:

- what physical relationship it changes;
- why it is justified by current developmental opportunity evidence;
- what developmental runway it buys;
- what biological pressure remains;
- how it could accidentally trivialize the ecology.

==================================================
PREFERRED ORDER OF INTERVENTION
==================================================

Prefer the smallest number of interpretable changes.

Start conceptually with:

1. greater encounter opportunity through arena/source geometry;
2. enough energy runway for useful repetition;
3. enough integrity resilience that increased contact frequency does not
   simply kill or cripple the organism before learning;
4. preserve real damage and restoration as meaningful consequences.

Do not immediately change:

- P learning rates;
- association strength;
- cortical rules;
- chemistry strength;
- sensor sensitivity;
- source semantic identity;
- motor-learning mechanism.

Those remain mechanism/signal hypotheses, not first nursery dials.

Source uptake/capacity/renewal should also remain unchanged initially
unless the design shows that increased encounter rate alone still cannot
produce meaningful positive energy consequences.

==================================================
RESILIENCE DOES NOT MEAN REMOVING INTEGRITY
==================================================

The integrity analysis is encouraging specifically because bodily damage
is already reaching learned regulation.

Do not remove that pathway by making impacts inconsequential.

A good nursery should instead favour:

MORE SURVIVABLE CONSEQUENCES,
NOT STRONGER CONSEQUENCES.

Prefer:

- repeated mild/moderate impacts;
- recoverable integrity deficits;
- continued sensing and actuation after injury;
- real opportunities for later re-approach;
- ordinary repair opportunity.

Avoid a configuration where one unlucky collision effectively ends the
developmental experiment.

Also avoid increasing mover speed or damage severity merely to create more
"training events".

==================================================
PRESERVE SPACE FOR LEARNED BEHAVIOUR
==================================================

A smaller arena still needs enough geometry for different strategies to
exist.

Retain meaningful room for:

- approach;
- retreat;
- turning away;
- waiting;
- detour;
- wall following;
- gentler contact;
- stable low-stress contact;
- leaving one source and reaching another;
- revisiting a depleted/recovering source.

Do not shrink the world until all trajectories become forced collisions
or immediate source contact.

==================================================

ENERGY OPPORTUNITY:
- first source-contact timing;

- departure/revisit;
- same-source vs different-source recurrence;
- positive AND negative E-credit opportunities;

- I-credit opportunities;

EXPLORATION:
