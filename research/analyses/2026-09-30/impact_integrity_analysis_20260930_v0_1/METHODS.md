# Passive impact/integrity analysis methods

Jason's attached request authorizes a new passive analysis of existing evidence. This is not an execution authority. No world/field step, birth, continuation, alternative physical trajectory, founder selection or nursery change is permitted. Production code and all previous records remain read-only. New files are confined to this analysis directory.

## Fixed descriptive choices, before comparing severity trends

Audit the sealed 60-row denominator and sum actual damage from every available event stream, including only FS-060's durable prefix. Analyze every positive-damage life, not merely the nine named examples.

Retain every contact record. Group records separately by collider into behavioral episodes. A new episode requires BOTH at least 0.1 s without recorded contact with that collider AND an intervening recorded native-endpoint surface clearance greater than 0.05 world units (10% of the 0.5 body radius). Exact episode onset/end are the first/last event times; elapsed span and actual contact duration are separate. Zero-impulse touches are retained in the raw contact table, not counted as independent encounters. This grouping is descriptive, not a new physical law. Report fixed clearance sensitivities 0.01 and 0.10 as well as 0.05, and retain temporal contact bouts separated by >0.1 s so long near-surface episodes can be inspected without pretending every solver release is a new encounter. No threshold is selected for a favorable trend.

Use the first damaging episode, every later same-collider episode, and every other collider episode. Preserve single-episode limitations. Compare actual severity components separately: closing normal velocity, tangential velocity, impulse, sustained force, damage, and drive. Instantaneous impulse has no finite peak force in this model; do not divide it by an invented duration. Sustained force is the recorded impulse/duration. There is no contact area or pressure unit, so do not manufacture stress/pressure.

Native endpoints give actual velocity, angular velocity, commands, forces and E/I. Positive-duration event positions obey the implemented endpoint update p_end=p_start+duration*v_end; angle follows the analogous identity. Recover subinterval velocity by this algebra only. At zero-duration impacts, add the recorded normal impulse/mass to obtain post-impact velocity. No dynamics are integrated. Retain floating-point displacement uncertainty; mark unreconstructable velocities rather than guessing. Reset the algebra to the recorded native endpoint each native interval and verify agreement within its numerical bound. Contact-surface tangential velocity includes body rotation. Relative normals use the recorded contact normal and the collider's analytic prescribed velocity at the contact-record timestamp.

For encounter rates, report time and native-polyline path before first injury and afterward, plus time within 0.25 and 1.0 surface-gap bands. These are realized exposures, not estimates of a learned impact probability. Inspect every fixed 30-second age block after first injury for the nearest recorded approach to each contacted collider, including blocks with no contact. Moving geometry and decreasing E remain confounders. Distances/path are sampled endpoint measures; exact collision times are event measures.

## Internal alignment and receiver probes

Align every damage/restoration event to its previous and next actual handoff. Keep integrity trend, eligibility, learning term, spring/reference term, applied I-bank update, I reference, learned-I output, exploration, actual-body and associative/evoked inputs distinct. Existing exact full-wave operand files are reused where available.

Predetermine probes for (a) the 0.2 s immediately preceding every behavioral-episode onset, (b) the preceding 0.2 s at every temporal contact-bout onset, and (c) the 0.2 s ending at the closest native approach in each fixed 30 s post-injury age block for every contacted collider. Keep every probe, not just favorable ones. Deduplicate computation, retain each window identity. Use first/pre-injury observations and earliest roster-order no-damage records at the same wave age as descriptive comparisons. If no eligible comparator exists, mark unavailable.

Detached P reconstruction uses the frozen existing replay reader with fields=False and deep=False, checking commands, actual waves, RNG and crossed P endpoints. It consumes recorded inputs and never calls world/field evolution. For the local receiver, omit only learned-I from its held regulator output; keep need, features, E contribution, exploration and all other state fixed. Report support, current and attenuation differences separately. Feed the changed current/attenuation through one frozen motor receiver with the same input terms and starting tendency. Do not propagate altered support through cortex/history, and do not integrate a body response. Compare normal versus omitted signed command pair, actuator-force algebra, normal component of commanded force and angular-acceleration tendency. A favorable sign is only an immediate direction, not proof of later benefit; do not treat a norm as direction.

Report every damaged life in the requested descriptive categories, with explicit avoidance and softening columns and no combined scalar score. Nursery implications are recommendations for Jason's review only. Do not alter Nursery-0 or increase hazards.
