==================================================
TWO DISTINCT QUESTIONS
==================================================

Keep these separate.

AVOIDANCE:

Did later behavior reduce the probability/exposure to impact?

Possible evidence:
- turn-away before later contact;
- increased minimum clearance;
- longer recontact interval;
- fewer contacts per unit path/time after injury;
- retreat rather than continued approach;
- changed route around the same structure.

SOFTENING:

Did later contacts occur with lower physical severity?

Possible evidence:
- reduced normal impact velocity;
- reduced impulse;
- reduced peak/mean force;
- reduced damage;
- more tangential/oblique approach;
- reduced actuator drive before contact;
- shorter damaging engagement;
- transition toward gentle sustained contact.

Do not combine these into one scalar score.
