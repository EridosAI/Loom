==================================================
OBSERVED EVIDENCE
==================================================

Use NEWBORN_EXPLORATION_DYNAMICS_AUDIT_v0_1 as the primary evidence.

Across 59 complete current-world lives:

- median path length ≈ 13.6558 world units;
- median maximum excursion from birth ≈ 0.5327;
- median final displacement ≈ 0.2924;
- median sustained forward/reverse switches ≈ 94 per life;
- median forward and reverse bout duration ≈ 3.4 s;
- approximately equal forward and reverse residence;
- median re-entry into previously visited 0.25-unit cells ≈ 92.96%;
- coverage growth largely stalls while motion continues;
- body heading remains strongly persistent;
- movement direction commonly reverses relative to that heading;
- 50 contact-free lives show essentially the same pattern;
- therefore wall/contact trapping does not explain the population result.

The current spontaneous motor implementation contains independent
zero-centred components with periods 7 s and 9 s plus short-correlated
noise.

The existing 7/9-s oscillator bases show approximately R² ≈ 0.93 against
the corresponding recorded command channels.

The joint 7/9-s periodic template repeats every 63 s. Actual trajectories
do not repeat exactly because other terms and noise remain active, but
this fixed recurrent template should be considered when interpreting the
observed repeated forward/backward movement.

==================================================
CURRENT INTERPRETATION
==================================================

The leading descriptive limitation is not simple lack of motor activity.

It is:

SUBSTANTIAL MOVEMENT
+
SHORT-LIVED FORWARD/REVERSE TRANSLATION
+
STRONGLY PERSISTENT BODY HEADING
+
HIGH SPATIAL RECURRENCE
=
POOR EXPLORATORY EXPOSURE.

Do not silently convert this interpretation into a causal proof.

The passive audit cannot isolate every contribution from:

- spontaneous generator;
- direct sensory feedback;
- P regulation;
- body mechanics;
- depletion-dependent actuation.

However, the evidence is strong enough to justify reviewing the newborn
spontaneous temporal structure before treating smaller arena geometry as
the primary remedy.

==================================================
DESIGN QUESTION
==================================================

Ask:

What is the minimum blind endogenous motor dynamic that gives a newborn
meaningful exposure to its environment while leaving WHAT IS USEFUL to
be learned from experience?

The desired newborn should naturally generate enough spatial variation
for reality to teach it.

It should not be born knowing where to go.

==================================================
FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS TO EXAMINE
==================================================

Consider whether the spontaneous motor process should provide:

1. sustained translational bouts long enough to produce meaningful
   displacement;

2. slower changes in directional tendency than the present ~3.4-s
   forward/reverse bouts;

3. gradual wandering / curvature;

4. occasional larger spontaneous reorientation;

5. variation across time and individuals;

6. no exact short-period repeating motor template;

7. no guaranteed spatial coverage;

8. ordinary opportunities for stopping, reversing and low activity;

9. continued compatibility with paired low-level actuation;

10. continued susceptibility to sensory feedback and learned regulation.

In particular examine whether the spontaneous process should be an
ONGOING ACTIVITY that learned regulation can materially bias rather than
a large fixed oscillator onto which learned signals make only tiny
perturbations.

Do not assume this requires increasing actuator magnitude.

==================================================
IMPORTANT DISTINCTION
==================================================

Separate:

MOTOR MAGNITUDE
from
MOTOR TEMPORAL ORGANISATION.

The existing animals already accumulate substantial path length.

Increasing amplitude alone may:

- increase energy consumption;
- increase impact severity;
- preserve the same forward/back cancellation;
- shorten developmental runway.

Prefer improving exploratory displacement per unit of ordinary movement
before simply increasing strength.

Remain open to a better unfamiliar construction.

==================================================
LOOM-SPECIFIC CHECK
==================================================

For each candidate ask:

Does this create useful ongoing activity that EXPERIENCE can shape?

A spontaneous mechanism that generates excellent random coverage but
leaves little room for learned influence is not necessarily suitable.

Likewise a mechanism that requires P to already know how to move is not
suitable.

The target is a newborn predisposition that exposes itself to reality,
then becomes increasingly shaped by what happens.
