# Behavioural development watchlist v0.1

**Research-analysis aid, not canonical Loom doctrine, a founder score or a pass/fail rubric.** This first application uses the preserved **NON-CANONICAL M1 RESURRECTION SANDBOX**. No simulation or intervention is authorized by this document.

The question is whether experience is followed by changing physical behaviour, after accounting for changing opportunities and body capability. Observed change, possible developmental signal and causal evidence are different statuses. A measured change is not automatically learning; no change is not automatically failure.

Each row below specifies the phenomenon, developmental rationale, observer quantity, denominator, major confounds, passive availability and current evidence. “Measured” refers to this package, “proxy” to the stated approximation, and “unresolved” to interpretation rather than a missing life. Tables are indexed in [DATA_DICTIONARY.md](DATA_DICTIONARY.md); findings and biographies are in [the main report](FUNCTIONAL_EXPRESSION_OVER_TIME_v0_1.md).

The longitudinal dashboard always uses the same seven complete lives and the fixed bands 0–600, 600–1500, 1500–2500, 2500–3500 and 3500–4500 s. 008 is host-censored; 009–012 are pilots. Never treat them as missing-at-random 4500-s lives. Quarantined support still changes E and experience. Every behavioural family must retain E/I, speed, support proximity, exposure and missing-opportunity counts.

## Reuse rules and unresolved measurement work

Freeze observer definitions before comparing a new denominator; keep threshold sensitivities and unmatched cases. The current thresholds do not alter the organism and are not proposed scientific parameters. Retain source contacts and adverse impacts together; source contact alone must not select a preferred motor process, founder or mechanism.

Raw event ledgers are exact. Some watchlist quantities remain proxies: initial rather than whole-bout restorative speed; source retention anchored to productive contact onset rather than exact first-transfer time; rotation/path rather than geometric path curvature; visited-cell and contact sequences rather than route graphs. “Unrelated cells” means geometric cells traversed, not semantically unrelated places. These limitations are part of the current status and must travel with reused tables.

No-opportunity and right-censoring must be explicit. No causal learned-avoidance, learned-source-navigation or repair-seeking claim is accepted by this watchlist. Testing accumulated learned contributions against identical physical/RNG histories would require separately authorized branches; none is prepared or run here. Stop for Jason's review.
