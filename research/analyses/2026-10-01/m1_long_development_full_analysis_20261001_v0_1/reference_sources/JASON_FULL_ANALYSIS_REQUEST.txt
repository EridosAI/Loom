==================================================
B. ENERGY SELF-SUFFICIENCY AND RESURRECTION INTERVALS
Reconstruct every interval between energy resurrections.
For each interval report:
- start/end age;
- duration;
- source energy acquired;
- expenditure;
- number of source-contact bouts;
- productive-contact time;
- path;
- distinct sources;
- same-source revisits;
- final cause.
Ask:
Does the interval between required energy resurrections lengthen with
developmental age?
Does actual environmental energy replace an increasing fraction of
expenditure?
Does source energy per unit path/time increase?
Does transfer per source-contact bout increase?
Do not count the external support jump as energy skill.
==================================================
C. SOURCE ENCOUNTERS AS BEHAVIORAL BOUTS
Group solver/contact fragments into defensible behavioral source-contact
bouts using a declared geometry/time rule and sensitivity checks.
For every bout retain:
- source identity;
- onset/end;
- approach geometry;
- contact duration;
- transfer;
- source stock;
- departure;
- next source contact;
- next same-source contact;
- path/time until recontact;
- E state;
- relevant sensory state.
Distinguish:
- accidental brush;
- sustained productive contact;
- immediate jitter/recontact;
- true departure/revisit.
Test longitudinally whether later bouts become:
- longer;
- more productive;
- more likely to be revisits;
- reached with shorter search path/time;
- followed by reduced probability of immediate departure.
==================================================
D. NASCENT NAVIGATION / CHEMOTAXIS
Around source approaches and departures, analyze actual raw permitted
sensory history.
Ask whether developmental age changes relationships among:
- bilateral chemistry differences;
- chemistry magnitude/change;
- light/context;
- body-relative motion;
- motor command;
- turning direction;
- subsequent source-distance change.
Look for emergence of sensorimotor contingency such as:
"this motor pattern tends to increase this sensory pattern"
without introducing global source bearing as an organism feature.
Observer geometry may be used only to assess the realized outcome.
Compare early versus late episodes within the SAME organism.
Normalize source-contact rates by path/exposure so increased contact from
simply moving more is not mistaken for learned navigation.
==================================================
E. ENERGY-REGULATOR SPECIFICITY
This is high priority.
Determine what the learned E regulator is actually learning.
Decompose its inputs/weights/contributions by feature family where the
implementation permits:
- actual E;
- other body terms;
- sensory/cortical context;
- associative q/context;
- motor/history features.
Ask whether late learned-E output is:
1. mostly a general low-energy behavioural modulation;
2. context-specific to source-related sensory states;
3. motor-history specific;
4. materially influenced by association.
Compare source-approach/source-contact windows against age/E-matched
non-source windows.
A large E-bank norm alone is not evidence of source learning.
==================================================
F. LEARNED CONTROL AUTHORITY
Quantify over developmental age:
- M1 spontaneous RMS;
- direct-feedback RMS;
- learned E current;
- learned I current;
- associative motor evocation;
- attenuation;
- resulting command contribution.
Report ratios but do not create a scalar performance score.
In particular ask:
Does learned motor/regulatory influence increase relative to M1 with age?
Is the operating point locally sensitive enough that the learned term
could alter physical motion?
Or is useful information potentially present but behaviourally drowned
by the spontaneous prior?
==================================================
G. PASSIVE RECEIVER OMISSIONS

at selected event-aligned windows.
No world evolution.

- learned E omitted;
- learned I omitted;
- associative feature input omitted;
- motor evocation omitted.

- first productive source encounter;

- regulator controls;
- motor current;
- attenuation;
- immediate paired command;
- source-normal / movement-relevant command component where observer

This establishes immediate influence only, not alternate-world benefit.
==================================================

Analyze complete H trajectories rather than endpoint norms.
Report:
- writes;
- decay;

- norm;
- use;

Does repeated experience create associations that are subsequently read?

Do not modify H in this analysis.
